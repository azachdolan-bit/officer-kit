# Hill 40: the seam at night

Topic: Hill 40, defending the platoon seam against a night attack
Limit: 2:00   Pace: 140

## 1 · THE POST 0:00
At 0140 on Hill 40, a corporal on a listening post heard something in the dark. He said later, "We heard shovels before we heard anything else." He was sitting on the seam between two platoons, and that is where the enemy was digging in.

## 2 · THE ATTACK 0:21
At 0215 the attack came straight at that boundary. The platoons had tied in at dusk, and the post had already reported. The seam held. By the company's count, 37 enemy died in front of it. *(OPTIONAL CUT)*

## 3 · THE SEAM IN TIME 0:40
Then the flares ran out at 0300. The second attack came at 0410. Field Notes on Seams says it plainly: "The enemy looks for the seam in space and the seam in time." The first attack found a seam that was watched. The second waited for one that was dark.

## CLOSE 1:05
So watch both seams. The boundary with the next platoon, and the hour the lights go out.
