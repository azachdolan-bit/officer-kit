# Hill 40

Topic: Hill 40, defending the platoon seam against a night attack

The campaign that summer began with three weeks of movement across the valley.

## Background
The regiment had crossed the river in May with 900 Marines.

The weather was hot and the supply line was long, and the battalion had been reorganized twice.

## The night
Six Marines died that night when the attack came at 0215 — right on the seam.
