# Hill 40 night fight: after action notes (FICTIONAL, written for this harness)

On the night of 12 to 13 June, the enemy attacked Hill 40 at 0215, aiming at the boundary between 1st and 2d Platoons. The platoons had tied in at dusk and posted a listening post on the seam.

The listening post heard digging at 0140 and reported it. "We heard shovels before we heard anything else," the post's corporal said.

At dawn the company counted 37 enemy dead in front of the seam. The battalion log gives 45; the regimental summary gives "about 30". The attack's leader is named differently in two accounts.

Company casualties for the two days 12 and 13 June were 6 killed and 19 wounded. The notes do not break out the night alone.

Illumination ran out at 0300. The second attack came at 0410, after the flares stopped.
