# Hill 40: holding the seam at night

Topic: Hill 40, defending the platoon seam against a night attack

On Hill 40 the enemy attacked the platoon seam at night, and the seam held because the platoons tied in at dusk and put a listening post on it; the second attack came when the flares stopped.

## Context
Hill 40 was a company position with two platoons forward. The fight this brief covers is the night of 12 to 13 June.

## The seam was the target
The attack at 0215 aimed at the boundary between 1st and 2d Platoons. The listening post on that boundary heard digging at 0140, 35 minutes before the attack.

## The seam in time
Illumination ran out at 0300, and the second attack came at 0410.

## Lines not to say
- "Six Marines died that night." The 6 killed covers two days, 12 and 13 June.
- "Exactly 37 attackers died." The counts run from about 30 to 45; say "dozens" or "37 by the company's count".
- The leader's name. Two accounts disagree.
