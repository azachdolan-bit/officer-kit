# Command investigation: heat casualty on Range 400, 12 August 2026
Convening authority: Battalion commander   Convening order: 14 August 2026   Due: 13 September 2026   Investigating officer: Company executive officer   Classification: UNCLASSIFIED

## Preliminary statement
All reasonably available evidence was collected. The investigation concerned a heat casualty during a platoon live fire attack on 12 August 2026. No signed witness statements were taken because the incident involved personal injury; summaries of testimony are enclosed. A two day delay occurred while the range control log was obtained; no extension was requested. The corpsman's account and the RSO's account differ on the time the casualty was first reported; the range control log (enclosure (4)) was relied on as the more reliable record. Original evidence is held by the battalion S-1, contact enclosure (1).

## Findings of fact
1. On 12 August 2026, 1st Platoon, Company C conducted a live fire attack on Range 400 with 44 Marines under a company commander approved risk assessment worksheet with a residual level of IE. [Encl (2), (3)]
2. The risk assessment worksheet directed hourly flag condition checks by the RSO and a work rest cycle per the flag. [Encl (3)]
3. The range control log records a black flag at 1300. [Encl (4)]
4. Serial 3 began at 1345 and continued without a rest cycle until 1440. [Encl (4), (5), (6)]
5. At approximately 1440, <SUBJECT>, a rifleman in 2d squad, collapsed during the assault; the corpsman treated him at the entry control point and he was transported to the base clinic, arriving at 1512. [Encl (5), (7), (8)]
6. <WITNESS 1>, the RSO, stated in summary that the 1300 flag check was not made because he was on the firing line for the serial 2 shakedown. [Encl (6)]

## Opinions
1. That the heat casualty was caused by the continuation of serial 3 under black flag without the work rest cycle the risk assessment required. [FF 2, 3, 4, 5]
2. That the RSO's hourly flag check, a control on the signed worksheet, was not performed at 1300. [FF 2, 3, 6]
3. That the risk assessment placed the flag check and the line safety duties on the same person during a serial, which made the check likely to be missed. [FF 2, 6]

## Recommendations
1. That the company amend its range standing operating procedure to assign flag condition checks to a person not on the firing line during a serial. [Op 3]
2. That the battalion consider non punitive corrective action for the RSO as the commanding officer deems appropriate, in light of opinions 1 and 2. [Op 1, 2]
3. That the corpsman's actions be recognized by letter of appreciation. [Op 1]
