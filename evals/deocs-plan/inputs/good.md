## Findings
1. Report section Response Rate: 287 invited, 221 responded, as the report states them.
2. Report section Risk Factors, item Workplace Hostility: 24 percent of respondents marked at least one item unfavorably; the report's comparison figure is 15.

4. Report section Locally Added Questions, item 1 (maintenance shift schedule): 9 of 12 short answer comments raise the same subject, recorded as a count, not quoted.
