# Meritorious Mast
Marine: <MARINE>, Corporal, motor transport dispatcher   Unit: motor transport platoon, headquarters and service company, infantry battalion   Awarding officer: Commanding Officer, infantry battalion   Date: 18 September 2026
Reported by: Motor Transport Officer, headquarters and service company, who observed the performance
References: MCO 1650.19J w/Ch 1 enclosure (2) paragraph 8.g; NAVMC 10935

## Level check
Recognition: Meritorious Mast
The performance is beyond the usual requirements of the dispatcher's billet and shows judgment and initiative under 8.g: the Marine caught a dispatch error no one had tasked the Marine to look for and reworked the plan before the convoy departed. The magnitude stays inside the platoon for a single day and the Marine acted at the level of responsibility of the billet held (enclosure (2) paragraph 1), so the facts do not reach the Certificate of Commendation step. No Letter of Appreciation, Certificate of Commendation, or personal decoration recognizes or is pending for this service; 8.g(1) does not bar the Mast: "A Meritorious Mast shall not be conducted when the service or performance of the Marine is recognized through the awarding of a Letter of Appreciation, Certificate of Commendation, or a personal decoration."

## Facts
- 27 August 2026, 0430 to 0615: <MARINE> was the duty dispatcher for a 16 vehicle convoy carrying 2 rifle companies to a range complex.
- At 0440 <MARINE> found that 3 of the 16 vehicles on the dispatch had licensing expirations dated before 27 August 2026 for the operators assigned.
- <MARINE> pulled the 3 operators, reassigned 3 licensed operators from the maintenance section, and rewrote the dispatch by 0530, 45 minutes before the 0615 departure.
- The convoy departed on time at 0615 with 16 vehicles and no unlicensed operator; the platoon's previous 2 convoys that quarter (July 2026 and early August 2026) had each departed late, by 20 and 35 minutes.
- The motor transport officer confirmed that checking operator licenses against the dispatch is not part of the dispatcher's turnover and that <MARINE> was not tasked to do it.

## Text
Corporal <MARINE> is commended for judgment and initiative as duty dispatcher on 27 August 2026. Preparing a 16 vehicle convoy to move 2 rifle companies to a range complex, Corporal <MARINE> checked the operator licenses against the dispatch, a check that is not part of the dispatcher's turnover, and found 3 operators whose licenses had expired. The Marine pulled the 3 operators, reassigned 3 licensed operators from the maintenance section, and rewrote the dispatch by 0530, 45 minutes before departure. The convoy left on time at 0615 with 16 vehicles and no unlicensed operator, after the platoon's 2 previous convoys that quarter had each left late. Corporal <MARINE>'s check kept 2 rifle companies on their training schedule and kept the platoon inside its own licensing rules.
