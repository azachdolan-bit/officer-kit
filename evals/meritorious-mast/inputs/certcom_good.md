## Level check
Recognition: Certificate of Commendation
The facts exceed a Meritorious Mast on both of the order's factors (enclosure (2) paragraph 1): magnitude, because the platoon's work stood up a water point that 5 companies drew from for 11 days, and level of responsibility, because the Marine ran the platoon without a platoon commander for the period. They do not reach a personal decoration: the effect stayed inside one battalion's exercise, the comparison is to the same platoon's last exercise, and the achievement was not recognized beyond the battalion. Because a Certificate of Commendation recognizes this service, no Meritorious Mast is held for it: "A Meritorious Mast shall not be conducted when the service or performance of the Marine is recognized through the awarding of a Letter of Appreciation, Certificate of Commendation, or a personal decoration" (8.g(1)).

## Facts
- 8 June 2026 to 18 June 2026: <MARINE> led the engineer platoon of 24 Marines through the battalion field exercise; the platoon commander billet was vacant for the whole period.

## Text
For commendable performance of duty as platoon sergeant of an engineer platoon from 8 June 2026 to 18 June 2026. Leading 24 Marines with the platoon commander billet vacant for the whole period, Staff Sergeant <MARINE> established the battalion's water point on 9 June 2026, 1 day after arriving, and produced 62,000 gallons of potable water over 11 days for 5 companies. At the same exercise in June 2025 the platoon had needed 3 days to establish the point and produced 41,000 gallons over 10 days. The point recorded 0 water quality test failures against 4 the year before. Staff Sergeant <MARINE> also taught 2 classes on the purification unit to 9 operators from the supported companies. The battalion drew clean water from the first full day of the exercise to the last.
