# Orchard Frost Report (OFR): Grower Handout (FICTIONAL, written for this harness)

Send the OFR to the Valley Frost Desk whenever a probe reads at or below 1 degree.

Line A. PROBE: probe number, three digits.
Line B. TEMP: lowest reading, to the nearest half degree; a value exactly on a quarter rounds UP.
Line C. STAGE: bloom stage, one of TIGHT, PINK, FULL, or PETAL-FALL.
Line D. ACTION: FANS, WATER, or NONE.

Rule: at STAGE TIGHT, readings above minus 2 are reported with ACTION NONE.

Worked example: probe 014 reads minus 1.25 at FULL bloom, fans started. Report: PROBE 014, TEMP minus 1.0, STAGE FULL, ACTION FANS.
