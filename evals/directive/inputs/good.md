## Body
1. Situation. To set the procedure by which the company builds, approves, and publishes its weekly training schedule within the timeline that reference (a) sets for the battalion.

2. Mission. The company operations chief, the platoon commanders, and the executive officer produce a signed weekly training schedule on enclosure (1) two weeks before the training week so that each platoon knows its events, times, places, uniforms, and instructors before the week begins.

3. Execution

    a. Commander's Intent and Concept of Operations

        (1) Commander's Intent. Every event on the schedule has a named instructor by billet, a place, a uniform, and a gear list, and no event appears that has not been resourced. End state: the schedule the Marines read on Monday is the schedule the company executes, and changes after publication are the exception recorded with a reason.

        (2) Concept of Operations. The platoon commanders submit their platoon events to the operations chief by the deadline in paragraph 3c(1); the operations chief consolidates them on enclosure (1) and resolves conflicts over ranges, vehicles, and classrooms; the executive officer reviews the draft against the battalion schedule and reference (a); the company commander signs.

    b. Subordinate Element Missions

        (1) Company operations chief. Main effort. Consolidates platoon inputs, confirms ranges, vehicles, and classrooms with the battalion operations section, and delivers the draft to the executive officer.

        (2) Platoon commanders. Supporting effort. Submit their events with instructor, place, uniform, and gear list, and confirm each instructor is qualified for the event.

        (3) Executive officer. Reviews the draft for conflicts with the battalion schedule and for events without resources, and returns it to the operations chief with corrections. Reserve: the company gunnery sergeant assumes the operations chief's duties when the operations chief is absent.

    c. Coordinating Instructions

        (1) Platoon inputs are due to the operations chief by 1200 on the Wednesday three weeks before the training week.

        (2) The draft is due to the executive officer by 1200 on the Friday three weeks before the training week and to the company commander for signature by 1600 on the following Wednesday.

        (3) A change to a published schedule is approved by the company commander or the executive officer and posted with the reason and the date of the change.

4. Administration and Logistics

    a. Signed schedules and their changes are kept by the operations chief for the current fiscal year and the year before it, and disposed of per reference (b).

    b. Range, vehicle, and classroom requests are submitted through the battalion operations section on the battalion's forms; the operations chief keeps a copy with the schedule.

5. Command and Signal

    a. Command. This Order is applicable to all Marines and Sailors assigned or attached to the company.

    b. Signal. This Order is effective the date signed.
