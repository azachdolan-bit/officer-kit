## Body
1. Purpose. To direct a one time wall to wall inventory of serialized equipment before the fiscal year closes, and to set the report format and date.

2. Background. Reference (a) requires a quarterly inventory by each company. The fiscal year close out requires one battalion wide count reconciled against the consolidated memorandum receipt on a single date.

3. Action

    a. Company commanders inventory all serialized equipment on the company account between 1 and 5 September 2026 and report the count to the battalion supply officer on enclosure (1) by 1600 on 8 September 2026.

    b. The battalion supply officer reconciles the company reports against the consolidated memorandum receipt and reports the result to the commanding officer by 15 September 2026.

    c. A discrepancy of any serialized item is reported to the executive officer the day it is found.

4. Reserve Applicability. This Bulletin is applicable to all companies of the battalion, including the attached Reserve detachment.
