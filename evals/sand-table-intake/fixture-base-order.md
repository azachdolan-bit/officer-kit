# Fixture base order (fictional, for the harness only)

Copy no. 1 of 3. A fictional company defense order to a platoon, on the sketch sheet's MG grid square. Nothing in it is a real unit, person, place or exercise.

General Situation. You are the 2nd Platoon Commander, Bravo Company, 2/9. The battalion holds the town of Verlan, 4 km north of the Verlan Gap. Bravo Company holds the southern arc of the perimeter in the Gap.

Orientation. Rolling ground rising to the northwest; scrub and grass, cover only in the draws and on the slopes. Visibility 400 m from the low ground, 2 km from the hill tops. Three hills: Hill 310, Hill 295, Hill 270. Fenwick Trail runs north to south through the Gap, foot traffic only. Route Blue runs west to east, trafficable to wheeled vehicles.

1. Situation
    a. Enemy
        (1) Composition, disposition, strength. Squad sized elements of the Northern Front are staging in the AO for an attack to retake Verlan. A squad was seen at 2100 in a harbor site IVO 848 116. Armed with rifles, one medium machine gun per squad, and 82mm mortars on call.
        (2) Capabilities and limitations. Capable of a squad sized deliberate attack with mortars; limited by no reinforcement inside two hours. Capable of withdrawing south after 50 percent casualties; limited by the trail as the only route.
        (3) EMLCOA. The enemy seizes Hill 310 IOT open the Gap for a company attack on Verlan. He patrols tonight to find our gaps, then attacks the weakest position under mortar fire.
    b. Friendly
        (1) Higher. NLT 1900, 2/9 protects Verlan IOT allow relief operations to continue.
        (2) Adjacent. 1st Platoon blocks south IVO 845 128 (west of you). 3rd Platoon blocks south IVO 866 123 (east of you).
        (3) Supporting. Company 60s in general support from 852 131, priority of fires 2nd, 1st, 3rd.
    c. Attachments and detachments. One medium machine gun squad attached to each platoon effective immediately.
2. Mission. NLT 1900, Bravo Company blocks south of Verlan IOT deny the enemy freedom of movement into the town.
3. Execution
    a. Commander's intent. Purpose: deny the enemy the Gap. Method: platoon battle positions with interlocking fires and final protective fires. End state: enemy destroyed forward of the positions, the Gap held.
    b. Concept of operations. Scheme of maneuver: platoons develop their own. Fire support plan: EFST 1, disrupt the enemy attack IOT let the platoons destroy him in their engagement areas; method 60s, three targets per platoon, numbers from the FST; effects, the enemy cannot mass above a squad.
    c. Tasks
        (1) 2nd Platoon, main effort. NLT 1900, block south IVO Hill 310 IOT deny the enemy freedom of movement into Verlan.
        (2) 1st Platoon, supporting effort 1. NLT 1900, block south IVO 845 128.
        (3) 3rd Platoon, supporting effort 2. NLT 1900, block south IVO 866 123. BPT assume main effort.
    d. Coordinating instructions
        (1) Timeline. 0800 company order. 0900 back briefs. 1000 rehearsals. 1200 depart assembly area. 1400 leaders recon. 1700 fire plan sketches due. NLT 1900 defend.
        (2) Engagement criteria: platoons develop.
        (3) TCMs: company assembly area is the town of Verlan, 851 135. Platoons develop the rest.
4. Administration and logistics. CASEVAC: platoons develop; company CCP at 852 133. Available: 4 green smokes, 2 white star clusters, 5 radios, 800 rounds per machine gun.
5. Command and signal. Platoons write the signal plan. Company CP with the 60s at 852 131.
