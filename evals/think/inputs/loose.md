# Company standing operating procedure: duty

Marines assigned to duty, who have been designated by the platoon sergeant in accordance with the roster published each month by the company gunnery sergeant, will report at the appropriate time.

The duty NCO will be briefed as required. Vehicles will be inspected on a regular basis and any discrepancies will be reported as soon as possible.

This ensures accountability. The MTOE and the CMR will be reconciled and/or verified by several personnel prior to the conduct of an inspection of the armory.

Marines should submit the log. Marines must maintain the space. Marines will attend the brief.
