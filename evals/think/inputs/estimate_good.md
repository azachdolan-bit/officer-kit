# Estimate: Risk Assessment Worksheet, platoon live fire, 12 August 2026

Tier: deliberate. People can be hurt and the approval level follows from the arithmetic.

## Task and outcome
A Risk Assessment Worksheet for the 12 August platoon live fire attack, to get the event approved at the right level and to give the OIC and RSO something to supervise from. The worksheet is the right product; if the aim were only approval, the shorter path would be to reuse last quarter's sheet, which the order allows for a repeated event only once every assessment is re verified.

## Standard
MCO 5100.29C Volume 2, paragraphs 030302 and 040302 to 040304, read from the library. Figures 3-4 and 4-2 confirmed. The base range SOP is not on disk and nothing here is verified against it.

## Facts
- 44 Marines, three serials, Range 400, August, unimproved access road, night movement (from the user).
- 6,400 rounds 5.56 and 800 rounds 7.62 (from the ammunition request the user pasted).

## Assumptions
- The flag condition in August will require a work rest cycle. Becomes a fact from the range control log on the morning of. If false, nothing changes, because the control is a check performed either way.
- The two seven tons are available at 0530. Confirmed by the motor transport chief. If false, the movement hazard changes and the timeline slips one hour.

## Questions
- What went wrong or nearly went wrong the last time this range was run? Changes the hazard list, which nothing mechanical can check for completeness.
- Is the corpsman confirmed present for all three serials, with a certification date? Changes the medical plan and two residual levels. Blocking.
- Does the battalion publish its own matrix or worksheet form? Changes the levels and the approval line.

## Will not do
This does not decide what risk the commander accepts, and it does not certify the OIC or RSO. It computes levels from the order's matrix and names the approval authority that follows.

## Checked by
raw_check.py for the arithmetic, the flags, the approval line, and the four required elements; the red team agent for the hazard list. Nothing mechanical can tell you the hazard list is complete or that the people named will be there.
