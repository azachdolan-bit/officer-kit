# Company standing operating procedure: duty

The duty noncommissioned officer reports to the command post not later than 0740 on every duty day. The platoon sergeant publishes the roster by the 25th of the month prior.

The duty noncommissioned officer inspects the four section vehicles each Thursday and reports discrepancies to the motor transport chief the same day.

The armory custodian reconciles the consolidated memorandum receipt against the property record on the first duty day of each month. The company executive officer verifies that reconciliation within three duty days.
