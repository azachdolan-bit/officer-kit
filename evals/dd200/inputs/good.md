# DD 200: AN/PVS-14 lost 12 August 2026
Appointing authority: Battalion commander   Investigating officer: Company executive officer

## Property
| Nomenclature | NSN | Serial | Qty | Unit price | Total | Record | Custodian (label) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monocular night vision device AN/PVS-14 | 5855-01-432-0524 | 0000001 | 1 | $3,400.00 | $3,400.00 | Sub custody card 14 | <WITNESS 1>, squad leader |

## Findings of fact
1. On 14 July 2026 the quarterly inventory recorded one AN/PVS-14, NSN 5855-01-432-0524, serial 0000001, on sub custody card 14 to <WITNESS 1>. [Encl (2), (3)]
2. On 12 August 2026 the platoon returned from Range 400 and the item was not turned in to the armory; the armory custodian's log shows no receipt. [Encl (4)]
3. On 13 August 2026 <WITNESS 1> reported the item missing to the platoon sergeant, who notified the supply officer the same day. [Encl (5), (6)]
4. On 13 and 14 August 2026 the platoon searched Range 400, the vehicles, and the barracks; the search log records no result. [Encl (7)]
5. <WITNESS 1> stated in summary that the item was in an unsecured pouch on the vehicle during recovery from the range. [Encl (5)]
6. The unit standing procedure of 1 June 2026 requires night vision devices to be secured in the issued case and signed for at the armory before the vehicle departs the range. [Encl (8)]

## Opinions
1. That the item was lost between the range and the armory on 12 August 2026. [FF 2, 4, 5]
2. That the loss resulted from simple negligence as defined in reference (a), in that the item was not secured as the standing procedure required. [FF 5, 6]

## Recommendations
1. That <WITNESS 1> be held financially liable in the amount of the item's recorded value, subject to the rights the form provides. [Op 1, 2]
2. That the platoon add a night vision device count to the range recovery checklist before vehicles depart. [Op 1]
