## Approval authority: company commander per the command's matrix

## Emergency action plan
Nearest medical is the base clinic, 22 minutes by the range road; the corpsman stabilizes at the ECP; the RSO calls range control on the range net and the OIC calls the company on the admin net; the platoon sergeant drives.

## Cease training and training time out
Anyone calls "cease fire" by voice and the hand and arm signal; all weapons to Condition 4; the RSO confirms the line before any restart.

## Communications
Primary: range net   Secondary: company admin net, then cell to the company operations chief   Check: before each serial
