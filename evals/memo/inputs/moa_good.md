# Memo
Kind: Memorandum of Agreement

## Between
Marine Corps Base Training Support Center
Second Battalion, Fictional Marine Regiment

## Subj
SHARED USE OF THE INDOOR SIMULATED MARKSMANSHIP TRAINER

## Purpose
This agreement fixes how the two activities share the indoor simulated marksmanship trainer in Building 1140 during fiscal year 2027.

## Problem
Both activities schedule the trainer through separate calendars, and 14 of 62 reservations in fiscal year 2026 collided, costing an average of 3 hours of training each.

## Scope
This agreement covers reservation, cancellation, and damage reporting for the trainer in Building 1140. It does not cover the live fire ranges or the range maintenance fund.

## Agreement
1. The Training Support Center holds the single calendar and publishes it every Thursday by 1500.
2. The battalion submits reservations not later than 10 working days before the requested date, and cancels not later than 3 working days before, or forfeits its next reservation.
3. Either activity reports damage to the Training Support Center watch officer within 1 hour of discovery.

## Effective Date
1 October 2026
