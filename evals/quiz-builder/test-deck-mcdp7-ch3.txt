1. Victory goes to those who recognize the need to adapt, generate intelligent decisions, and do what?
   1. Execute them more quickly than the enemy
   2. Field the most modern equipment available
   3. Field the largest force in the theater
   4. Plan in more detail than the enemy
   Marked correct: 1

2. What does the chapter say is the single correct design for the ideal learning environment?
   1. A formal classroom led by a senior instructor
   2. Field exercises against a live opposing force
   3. Simulation training with recorded feedback
   4. None; objectives, Marines, and conditions differ
   Marked correct: 4

3. Recognition primed decision-making is an example of which kind of decision-making learning should also support?
   1. Deliberate, analytical decision-making
   2. Checklist-based procedural decision-making
   3. Rapid, experience-based decision-making
   4. Consensus-based team decision-making
   Marked correct: 3

4. Before replacing an existing instructional method with a new one, what must instructors first seek?
   1. Approval from the formal schoolhouse first
   2. Clear evidence the new method is better
   3. Learner feedback on the new method
   4. A trial run with one squad first
   Marked correct: 2

5. Rote memorization is efficient and useful for which kind of learning?
   1. Applying information in context
   2. Mastering a difficult skill
   3. Basic knowledge, such as standing orders
   4. Knowing when and how to deviate from doctrine
   Marked correct: 3

6. For the Marine learner, what are surfaces?
   1. Barriers such as ego, inexperience, and bias
   2. Enemy strengths to avoid
   3. Areas of weakness in knowledge
   4. Areas of existing understanding
   Marked correct: 4

7. Which individual barriers does the chapter say can lead to learning gaps?
   1. Personal ego, inexperience, and bias
   2. Fatigue, stress, and complacency
   3. Limited time, ranges, and equipment
   4. Rank, billet, and time in service
   Marked correct: 1

8. What does the chapter call Marines who seize the initiative to study their profession whenever and wherever they can?
   1. Warrior-leaders
   2. Warrior-scholars
   3. Student-practitioners
   4. Scholar-mentors
   Marked correct: 2

9. Which force-on-force design does the chapter use to test brilliance in the basics against an expected foe?
   1. A red force that adapts to blue force actions
   2. Complete free play between two opposing forces
   3. A red force scripted to use the enemy's tactics
   Marked correct: 3

10. What principle does the chapter draw from both the Macedonian phalanx and operations in Iraq and Afghanistan?
   1. Protect the force while accomplishing the mission
   2. Field more modern equipment than the enemy
   3. Wait for perfect information, then decide
   4. Mass heavy infantry at the decisive point of battle
   Marked correct: 1

11. In a learner-centric model, what is the main focus of effort?
   1. The instructor
   2. The learner
   3. The curriculum
   4. The commander
   Marked correct: 2

12. Where does the chapter say adaptive curriculum can be effective?
   1. At entry-level training and schools
   2. At formal schools of every level
   3. At intermediate and advanced schools
   Marked correct: 3

13. Self-directed learning is similar to which warfighting idea from MCDP 1?
   1. Main effort
   2. Combined arms
   3. Surfaces and gaps
   4. Mission tactics
   Marked correct: 4

14. Like mission tactics, what does self-directed learning give the learner?
   1. More ownership and autonomy
   2. A plan prescribed by the leader
   3. More structure and supervision
   4. Less accountability for results
   Marked correct: 1

15. Why is vicarious experience more important in the profession of arms than in others?
   1. Reading is more efficient than field training
   2. Simulation costs too much to use often
   3. Personal experience is unreliable in combat
   4. Marines cannot practice every variation before combat
   Marked correct: 4

16. Complete the adage on instructors: "Those who can, do, and those who ___."
   1. cannot do, teach
   2. have done well, teach
   3. have done it, lead
   4. have studied the most, teach
   Marked correct: 2

17. When do summative assessments identify the learning that occurred?
   1. After the learning activity
   2. Before the learning activity
   3. During the learning activity
   Marked correct: 1

18. Midway through a range week, a squad leader checks each Marine's progress to see what to strengthen. What type is this?
   1. Summative assessment
   2. Diagnostic assessment
   3. Formative assessment
   Marked correct: 3

19. Like war itself in MCDP 1, the learning environment is primarily based on what?
   1. Doctrine and publications
   2. Human characteristics
   3. Equipment and technology
   4. Facilities and ranges
   Marked correct: 2

20. During the stresses of combat, Marines will fight the way that they did what?
   1. Were equipped
   2. Were led
   3. Were ordered
   4. Learned
   Marked correct: 4
