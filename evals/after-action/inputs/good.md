# After action report: Platoon live fire attack
Unit: Company C   Dates: 12 August 2026   Location: Range 400   Prepared by: Platoon commander   Date: 13 August 2026

## Planned
Three serials of a platoon attack with live fire, 44 Marines, 0700 to 1500, 6,400 rounds of 5.56 and 800 of 7.62.

## What happened
### Setup
- 0630: range control opened the range 30 minutes late for a downrange sweep; serial 1 started at 0830, 30 minutes behind.
### Execution
- 0950: serial 1 complete with 2,100 rounds fired.
- 1410: company net dropped for 40 minutes when the relay vehicle moved to the ECP; serial 3 started 35 minutes late.
### Recovery
- 1610: brass and ammunition accountability closed with 47 rounds turned in; no casualties, no incidents.

## Sustain
| Observation | Why it worked | Keep it by |
|---|---|---|
| Dry rehearsal of the shift fire twice before each serial; no round outside a limit in 6,400 | Every fire team leader saw the limit before firing | Line in the OIC checklist |

## Improve
| Observation | Fix | Owner (billet) | By |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net dropped 40 minutes at 1410 when the relay moved | Stage the relay vehicle on the high ground before serial 1 and hold it until endex | Platoon sergeant | 26 August 2026 |

## Numbers
44 trained; 6,353 rounds fired; 8.5 hours; casualties none; equipment deadlined none.
