# sand-table-order tests

`fixture.sandtable.json` and `fixture.sandtable-brief.md` are a fictional platoon defense exported by the virtual sand table (every name and grid invented; no course material). `evals/sand_table_order_check.py` (28 items): the reader lays out every field, typed but unapplied text marked as such, 37 objects with 8 digit grids derived from UTM, a squad's sector of fire and a gun's FPL azimuth, three routes with computed legs, two parts, the AO corners, and the brief's checks; `good-order.md` is a lean order and passes the checker; `bad-order.md` fails on each planted defect (an invented grid, a warning not carried, an en dash, a dropped FPL azimuth, a task without a purpose, a target without its net) and reports its dropped position and field as advisory, which `--full` turns into failures. The harness also plants three defects in the good order: an EMLCOA with no tactical task and purpose, a block task with no condition, and a target with no net.

The good order doubles as the shape of the product: lean, grids copied from facts.md, TCMs one per line, movement as wavetops, EFST method as TTLODAC with the net, tasks by part, the two closing lists.
