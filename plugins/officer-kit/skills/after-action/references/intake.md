# After action intake

## 1. Frame
1. What was the event, when, where, for whom? Who reads this AAR and what will they do with it (plan the next one, brief the CO, fix a range)?
2. Does the unit have an AAR format? (Put it in the override file.)

## 2. Planned
3. What was the mission or purpose in one sentence? What was the timeline? What were the numbers: personnel, rounds, vehicles, hours, serials?

## 3. What happened, by phase
For each phase (movement, setup, execution by serial, night, recovery):
4. What happened, with a time and a number?
5. Where did it differ from the plan, and by how much?
6. What caused the difference, in terms the unit can act on? (A missing rehearsal, a late ammunition draw, an unlicensed driver; not "the weather.")

## 4. Sustain
7. What worked well enough that the next OIC should do it the same way? Why did it work? What keeps it working (a checklist line, a rehearsal, a person in a billet)?

## 5. Improve
8. What should change? The specific fix, not the topic ("draw ammunition the day prior and stage it at the ECP," not "improve logistics").
9. Who owns the fix, by billet? By when?

## 6. Numbers
10. Final counts: personnel trained, rounds fired, hours, casualties (say none), equipment deadlined, anything with a cost.

## What is not an observation
A complaint ("comm was bad"), a characterization ("the platoon was motivated"), or a judgment about a person. Ask for the time, the fact, the number.
