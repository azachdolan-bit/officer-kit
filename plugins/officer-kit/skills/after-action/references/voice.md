# After action voice

Observations: `<time>: <fact with number>; <difference from plan>`. "1410: company net dropped for 40 minutes when the relay vehicle moved; serial 3 started 35 minutes late."

Fixes: `<verb> <specific change>`: "stage the relay vehicle on the high ground before serial 1 and hold it until endex"; "draw ammunition the day prior and stage it at the ECP under the guard."

Verbs that make fixes real: stage, draw, rehearse, assign, post, publish, brief (only with a who and a when), inspect, license, mark, add, remove.

Strike: "improve communication," "better planning," "more attention to detail," "lessons were learned." Each is a topic, not a fix.
