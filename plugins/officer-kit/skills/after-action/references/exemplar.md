# After action exemplars, fictional

## Strong: platoon live fire attack

Planned: three serials of a platoon attack with live fire, 44 Marines, 0700 to 1500 on 12 August, 6,400 rounds of 5.56 and 800 of 7.62.

What happened. Setup: 0630, range control opened the range 30 minutes late for a downrange sweep; serial 1 started at 0830, 30 minutes behind. Execution: serial 1 complete 0950 with 2,100 rounds; serial 2 complete 1130; 1410, company net dropped for 40 minutes when the relay vehicle moved to the ECP, and serial 3 started 35 minutes late; serial 3 complete 1520. Recovery: brass and ammunition accountability closed at 1610 with 47 rounds turned in; no casualties, no incidents.

Sustain: the dry rehearsal of the shift fire twice before each serial (no round outside a limit in 6,400; keep it in the OIC checklist). The corpsman's ice sheets at the ECP (two Marines cooled during serial 2 and returned; keep the ice draw on the day prior list).

Improve: stage the relay vehicle on the high ground before serial 1 and hold it until endex (owner: platoon sergeant; by the next range, 26 August). Request the range open at 0600 for a 0700 start so a downrange sweep does not cost a serial (owner: platoon commander; on the next range request).

Annotation: every observation has a time and a number; every fix names a change, a billet, and a date; the causes are things the platoon controls.
