# Safety brief voice

Spoken, first person, short sentences. One hazard, one number or one event, one instruction. "Two Marines in the division died on motorcycles in May. Both were first rides after a deployment. If you have not ridden in six months, your first ride is with a mentor, on base, in daylight."

Strike: "be safe," "use good judgment," "remember that," "I cannot stress enough," "as always."
