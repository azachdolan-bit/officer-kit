# Safety brief intake

1. What is the occasion, when, and for whom? How long do you have? What does the command require in every brief? (Override file.)
2. What are the three hazards for this season and this unit? (August: heat, water, motorcycles. December: holiday driving, alcohol, home fires, money.) For each, what number can you source, or what happened recently in the unit or the division by type?
3. What has happened in the unit in the last quarter: mishaps, near misses, incidents, by type only?
4. What resources do Marines have, with the phone numbers as you have them: duty, the ride program, the chaplain, the base numbers?
5. What do you expect of them in one sentence each: a plan before leaving, a check in, calling for a ride?
6. Anything the CO or the sergeant major said this week that belongs in the brief?
