# Safety brief exemplar, fictional: 96 hour liberty, Labor Day

## The three things
1. Water: three drownings in the division's area in the last two summers, all at unguarded beaches, all with alcohol. Swim where there is a lifeguard, never alone, never after drinking.
2. Motorcycles: two riders in the wing hurt in the last quarter, both on the first ride after months off. First ride back is on base with your mentor in daylight, and your gear is the gear you were issued.
3. Driving: this is a 96 and the drive to the city is four hours; the division's last two fatalities were single vehicle, after midnight, driver asleep. Leave in the morning. If you are tired, stop; if you have been drinking, call the number below and the ride is free.

## Resources
- Company duty: (000) 000-0000
- Ride program: (000) 000-0001, any hour, no questions
- Chaplain: (000) 000-0002

## Expectations
- Your squad leader knows where you are going and who with before you leave.
- Check in with your squad leader Sunday by 1800.
- If anything goes wrong, you call your squad leader first, before anyone else.

## Required by the command
- Seat belts, every seat, every time.
- Fireworks are prohibited on base.

Annotation: three things, each with an event by type and an instruction; numbers the user supplied; under 250 words.

## Weak
"Be safe out there. Use good judgment. Don't drink and drive. Remember you represent the Marine Corps. Sergeant X had an incident last month, don't be like him." No number, no event by type, one name, and nothing anyone can act on.
