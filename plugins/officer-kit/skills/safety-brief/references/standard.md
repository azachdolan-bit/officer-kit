# Standard: MCO 5100.29C, Marine Corps Safety Management System (Vols 1 to 9, 2020 to 2021)

Volume 3, Traffic Safety: the motorcycle program (training, mentorship, the rider roster), the seat belt and driving rules, the holiday and long weekend emphasis. Volume 5, Recreation and Off Duty Safety: water, hiking, sports, alcohol, home hazards. Volume 9, Mishap Investigation and Reporting: what happens after, which is why the brief exists.

What the program expects a leader's brief to do: name the season's hazards; cover the command's required topics; make the ride program, the duty, and the resources known; set the expectation that a plan exists before liberty starts. The command's safety officer usually publishes required topics before a holiday; the override file carries them.

Verbatim extracts to add from the user's copy: the motorcycle mentorship requirement, the seat belt language, the recreation and off duty hazard list for the season.
