# DEOCS plan exemplars, fictional, annotated

Form only. Every number, date, count, section label, and phrase here is invented and is not to be taken into a product; `deocs_plan_check.py` fails a draft that lifts a sentence from this file. Read this after the intake is done and the fact list is confirmed, never before, so it teaches shape and supplies no answers. The user's own approved plans in `Reference/Exemplars/deocs-plan/` beat this file.

## Strong: battalion, annual DEOCS, four findings

```
# DEOCS command climate action plan
Unit: <UNIT> (a battalion), under a regiment, under a division   Survey: annual DEOCS   Opened: 8 September 2026   Closed: 3 October 2026
Out brief: 20 October 2026   Plan due: 5 November 2026   Commander: the battalion commander   Survey administrator: the servicing EOA (Collateral Duty EOA, JKO Prev 004 complete)
References: MARADMIN 306/25 paragraphs 1 to 3; MCO 5354.1G w/Admin Ch 1 enclosure (2) chapter 9 paragraphs 2, 4, 6, 7, 9, 11

## Findings
1. Report section Participation: 412 invited, 338 responded, as the report states them.
2. Report section Protective Factors, item Cohesion: 58 percent favorable; the report's comparison figure for the same item is 71.
3. Report section Protective Factors, item Leadership Support: 82 percent favorable; the highest item in the report. A strength to sustain.
4. Report section Locally Added Questions, item 2 (barracks maintenance response time): 14 of 19 short answer comments name the same subject, written as a count, not quoted.

## Actions
1. Action: schedule the 2027 survey window with two duty day blocks per company set aside for completion. Owner: the S-1 officer. Date: 1 August 2027. Measure: response count on the 2027 report compared to 338 of 412.
2. Action: run one focus group per company with the servicing EOA on the Cohesion item, then settle the follow on action at the commander's review. Owner: the servicing EOA, with each company commander. Date: focus groups by 20 November 2026; follow on action written by 4 December 2026. Measure: four focus group summaries on file with the EOA; the Cohesion item on the 2027 report compared to 58 percent.
3. Action: keep the monthly company commander time with each platoon on the training schedule. Owner: the S-3 officer. Date: on the schedule each month, first check 15 December 2026. Measure: 12 of 12 months on the training schedule; the Leadership Support item on the 2027 report compared to 82 percent.
4. Action: publish the barracks work order log with open and closed counts on the company boards each week. Owner: the battalion logistics officer. Date: first posting 13 November 2026. Measure: postings counted at the monthly review; the same locally added item on the 2027 report compared to this report.

## Debrief
Company formations during the week of 16 November 2026, each led by the company commander with the sergeant major present, from a one page summary the EOA prepares that carries the four findings and the four actions. The full report and the breakouts stay with the commander and the EOA.

## Reporting
Plan to the regimental commander by 5 November 2026 as set in the regiment's climate assessment SOP (recorded in Overrides/deocs-plan.md; no publication in the library sets this date). Completion of the annual DEOCS reported per reference (am) of the order, through the servicing EOA; the process is not in the library. Report and plan retained by the commander and the EOA for the next IGMCIP inspection (chapter 9 paragraph 11) and for the incoming commander's command team brief (chapter 2 paragraph 2.a).

## Not in the library
DoDI 6400.11 w/CH-1 (the out brief and plan timeline, recipient, and format); the portal's minimum respondent count for a breakout; the regiment's SOP is the user's, recorded in Overrides.
```

Annotation. The header carries the dates the checker tests: the survey opened before 31 October and closed inside 1 August to 30 November, and the out brief and plan due dates follow the close. Every finding names the report section and states the report's number as printed; finding 4 turns comments into a count and quotes none of them. Every action has the four parts and the owner is a billet. Every measure names what will be compared to what; none says which way it will go. Finding 3 is a strength, which the order's "protective and risk factors" makes a proper finding. The Reporting section says where the plan due date came from and marks the completion reporting process as not in the library rather than describing it from memory.

## Weak, annotated line by line

```
Out brief: TBD   Plan due: when the CO gets to it
```
[Not dates. The checker requires a day month year for both; nothing in the library sets how long after the close they may be, and the plan says so, but it still carries a date the commander can be asked about.]

```
1. Morale is low in the battalion because the junior Marines feel the SNCOs do not listen.
```
[Not what the report says. No section named, no number, and a reading of why. The survey is a perception data point and the order's tools for depth are focus groups and interviews; the plan records the report's item and number and leaves the "because" to those tools.]

```
2. The Sergeant who wrote about the motor pool was clearly describing Staff Sergeant [a name].
```
[Identifies a respondent and names a Marine. Both fail the checker. Short answer comments go on the plan as a theme with a count and never as a quotation or an inference about who wrote them.]

```
Action: leadership will re-emphasize the importance of cohesion. Owner: all hands. Date: ongoing.
```
[No action a person can start; "re-emphasize" cannot be checked on a date; "all hands" is not a billet; "ongoing" is not a date; no measure. Four failures in one line.]

```
Measure: cohesion will improve by the next survey and the climate will be fixed.
```
[A promise about the outcome. The measure names what will be compared; the next survey says what happened.]
