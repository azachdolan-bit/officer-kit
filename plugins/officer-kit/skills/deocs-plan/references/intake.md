# DEOCS plan intake

One question at a time. "I do not know" is recorded as a gap, never filled. Ask what this report raises, not what climate surveys usually raise. Never put a candidate answer inside the question. The report is the fact; the user reads the numbers off it and the tool writes them down as the report states them.

## 1. Frame
1. Which command is this plan for, by echelon (company, battalion or squadron, regiment or group, and the next up)? Who is the commander, by billet only?
2. Is this the annual DEOCS or the change of command CCA? For a change of command, on what date did the commander assume command? (The order: the change of command CCA within 90 days after assumption; the command team brief from the EOA within 30 days.)
3. On what dates did the survey open and close? (The MARADMIN: between 1 August and 30 November, commencing no later than 31 October; the order: extensions are not permitted.)
4. Who administered the survey, by billet: the servicing EOA, a Collateral Duty EOA, or the EOC? Did that person complete JKO Prev 004 before administering it? (If the unit calls the billet "EOR", the order removed that title; the plan says EOC.)
5. When is the out brief to the commander, and when is the plan due? Neither publication in the library sets those dates; who set them, and where is that written (higher headquarters SOP, the EOA's guidance, the portal)? Record the source in `Overrides/deocs-plan.md`.

## 2. The report
6. How many people were invited and how many responded, as the report states them? (Write the numbers down; do not compute a percentage the report does not print.)
7. Read each section of the report in the report's order. For each item the commander wants on the plan: what does the report say, in its own words or a close paraphrase, and which section and label is it under? One finding per item. Strengths the commander wants to sustain count as findings too; the order's objective names "protective and risk factors".
8. Does the report show any breakout (by grade band, by gender, by subunit) the commander wants to act on? Write it as the report shows it. If a breakout is small enough that people could be recognized, leave the breakout off the plan and act on the whole unit's result. The sources in the library set no minimum count; the portal guidance does, and it is not in the library.
9. Are there short answer or locally added questions? What the plan takes from them is the theme as a count ("11 comments on the same subject"), never a quotation that would let a reader place the writer.

## 3. The actions
10. For each finding: what will the command do about it, as one action a person can start on a date? Climb the ladder: what, for whom, by when, measured how.
11. Who owns it, by billet? (Company commander, sergeant major, S-3, EOA, chaplain. Never a name.)
12. By what date will it be done or first reviewed?
13. What will show it worked, as something that can be read off a document: the next DEOCS result on the same item compared to this one, an attendance roster count, a completed training entry in MCTIMS, a records review done on a date? The measure names what will be compared, never how it will come out.
14. Which other program specialists did or will the commander consult on this finding? (Chapter 9 paragraph 4 lists IPP, SJA, EEO, IG, Suicide Prevention, Substance Abuse, MCCS, chaplain, SARC.) Which of the order's other tools (interviews, focus groups, records reviews) will add depth before the action is settled?

## 4. Debrief and reporting
15. How and when will the unit be told what the survey found and what the command will do? (Formation, all hands, small groups by company, a posted summary.) What in the report stays with the commander and the EOA?
16. To whom does the plan go, and by when? What does the commander report about completion, and to whom? (The order requires the commander to report completion of the annual DEOCS per reference (am), and says an annual MARADMIN gives the process; the MARADMIN in the library gives none. The plan records what the user knows and marks the rest as a gap.)
17. Where will the plan and the report be retained, and who may see them? (Chapter 9 paragraph 10: requests for copies go through FOIA and PA; paragraph 11: the report is evaluated at IGMCIP inspections.)

## 5. The reader
18. How does this commander want to read the plan: a table, one paragraph per finding, or the order of the report? Record it in `Overrides/deocs-plan.md`.

## The quantification ladder
| Rung | Question |
|---|---|
| Action | What will the command do? |
| Scope | For whom, how often, how long? |
| Result | What will exist afterward that does not exist now? |
| Comparison | Against what: this report's number, last year's, the roster count? |
| Consequence | What does the commander read to know it happened? |
Stop at the rung the owner can start on Monday and the commander can check on the date.
