# MROW input exemplars, fictional, annotated

Form only. Every number, date, count, and phrase here is invented and is not to be taken into a product; `mrow_input_check.py` fails a draft that lifts a sentence from this file. Read this after the intake is done and the fact list is confirmed, never before, so it teaches shape and supplies no answers. The user's own approved MROW input in `Reference/Exemplars/mrow-input/` beats this file.

## Strong: company grade officer, annual report

```
# MROW input
MRO: <MARINE>, First Lieutenant, rifle platoon commander   Period: From 1 October 2025 To 30 September 2026   Occasion: AN
RS: company commander   RO: battalion commander   Prepared: 12 September 2026
References: MCO 1610.7B enclosure (2) chapter 1 paragraphs 6 and 7; chapter 2 paragraph 3.c; chapter 4 paragraphs 5 and 6

## Billet description
- Rifle platoon commander for 38 Marines and 1 corpsman; responsible for training to T&R standard, welfare, and readiness for deployment.
- Accountable for 52 serialized weapons and 14 items of communications equipment.
- Additional duty: company training officer from 3 November 2025; drafts the weekly training schedule for 3 platoons and the headquarters section.
- Special duty: range officer in charge for company live fire events, 4 planned for the period.

## Billet accomplishments
- Trained the platoon through 12 of 12 scheduled collective T&R events; 38 of 38 Marines current on annual requirements at the end of the period.
- Ran 4 company live fire ranges for 190 Marines with no mishap and no range control violation; each range package accepted by range control on first submission.
- Reduced the platoon's serialized gear discrepancies at the quarterly inventory from 6 in December 2025 to 0 in June 2026 and 0 in September 2026.
- Wrote 44 weekly training schedules for the company; 41 published by the battalion's Thursday deadline, against 29 of 44 the prior year.
- Deployed the platoon for 23 days to a combined arms exercise; the platoon completed 3 of 3 assigned attacks within the exercise controller's time standard.

## For the RS (not section C)
- PME: completed the nonresident Expeditionary Warfare School distance program, 14 August 2026.
- Commendatory material: none presented during the period.
- Promotion preference (item 8h): not applicable to this grade.
```

Annotation. The header puts the MRO's billet as the rules file states it, both dates, and both reporting officials by billet, so the RS can check the period against the report before reading a word. Section B bullets state duty, scope, and the standard the RS set, in present tense, with no adjective; the additional and special duties carry the date they were assigned, which is what paragraph 5.c(2) asks the RS to highlight. Section C bullets are results with a count and a baseline: 12 of 12, 41 of 44 against 29 of 44, 6 to 0. Nothing describes the MRO; nothing says how the RS should read it. The exercise attacks are stated against the controller's standard, not against another platoon. PME and commendatory material are in a separate list because chapter 1 paragraph 6.b says the summary is the place to tell the RS about them and paragraph 6.c(1)(c) keeps them out of section C. The "none" line for commendatory material is a fact the RS needs for item 6a; a blank would be a question.

## Weak, annotated line by line

```
- Outstanding platoon commander who ALWAYS puts his Marines first!!
```
[A superlative, uppercase for emphasis, exclamation, and a personal quality. Paragraph 6.c(3)(a) and (c) strike each of these, and paragraph 6.a(4) excludes the personal quality. There is no result in the line.]

```
- Awarded the Navy and Marine Corps Achievement Medal for the deployment.
```
[Paragraph 6.c(1)(c): "Do not address awards, other commendatory material, adverse material, or disciplinary action in section C." The presentation date goes in the list for the RS, who handles it in item 6a and section I.]

```
- Best platoon in the company, clearly ahead of [another platoon commander's name].
```
[A comparison to a named Marine, a superlative, and a name. The order asks for results against a standard or a prior number; another Marine's name never enters the input.]

```
- Has unlimited potential and should be marked in the top third of the RS's profile.
```
[Paragraph 6.a(4) excludes "potential impact"; the marks and the profile are the RS's (chapter 2 paragraph 3.c(3)). The MROW informs; it does not mark.]

```
- Sat as a member on the corporal selection board for 3 days in March.
```
[Paragraph 6.b Note: "Do not reference the MRO's participation as a member of a selection board or court-martial."]

```
- Worked through a difficult family situation during the deployment without letting it affect the platoon.
```
[Family content; paragraph 13.d(11) and the kit's blocked list. If it bears on the period the MRO tells the RS in person.]

```
Managed platoon training and did well.
```
[Not a bullet (5.c(1)(a) and 6.c(1)(a)); "did well" is a valuation with no count; "managed platoon training" is the duty, not a result.]
