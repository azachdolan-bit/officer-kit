# Standard: MCO 4400.201 w/Ch 3 (Management of Property in the Possession of the Marine Corps, 21 May 2025, 17 volumes); TECOM HQBN "How to fill out a DD 200 and NAVMC 6 (FLIPL how to)"

The base order (MCO 4400.201 CH-2, 2 Mar 2020, 11 pages, in the user's library at MCOs/04_Logistics/MCO_4400.201.pdf) lists the volumes: Volume 1, Accountability and Responsibility; Volume 4, Physical Inventory Control Program; Volume 17, Financial Liability Investigation of Property Loss (FLIPL). Volume 17 is the governing text for this tool and Volume 1 for the responsible officer's duties; neither volume's text is in the library yet. Download both from MCPEL into Reference and extract the negligence definitions and the DD 200 procedure into this file. The TECOM guide is a block by block walk of the DD 200 (Financial Liability Investigation of Property Loss) and the NAVMC 6 (cash sale or checkage).

## What the forms need (from the guide)
DD 200: the item (nomenclature, NSN, serial, quantity, unit price, total cost); the date of loss or discovery; the circumstances as a narrative; the actions taken to locate or recover; the responsible officer's block; the appointing authority's block; the investigating officer's findings and recommendation; the individual's rights when liability is recommended (legal advice, rebuttal). NAVMC 6: the Marine's election to pay, the amount, the pay account.

## Standards the opinion must name (extract verbatim from the user's copy)
Simple negligence; gross negligence; willful misconduct; proximate cause. The order defines them; the opinion quotes the definition it applies.

## Out of scope in this tool
Weapons, ammunition, classified, and controlled cryptographic items: separate reporting under their own orders.
