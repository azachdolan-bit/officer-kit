# DD 200 voice

Findings name the item by NSN and serial, the record, and the date. "On 14 July 2026 the quarterly inventory recorded one AN/PVS-14, NSN 5855-01-432-0524, serial 0000001, on sub custody card 14 to <WITNESS 1>, the squad leader of 2d squad. [Encl (3)]"

Opinions quote the standard. "That the loss resulted from simple negligence as defined in reference (a), in that <WITNESS 1> did not secure the item as the unit's standing procedure of 1 June 2026 required. [FF 4, 6, 7]"

Strike: "lost track of," "somehow," "must have," "probably," and any name.
