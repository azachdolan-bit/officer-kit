# DD 200 intake

1. What was lost or damaged: nomenclature, NSN, serial, quantity, unit price and total from the record? On what record is it carried (CMR, ECR, sub custody card) and who is the custodian (label)?
2. When was it discovered missing, by whom (billet), and how? When was the supply officer notified?
3. What search was done: where, when, by whom, with what result? Is there a search log or a missing gear statement?
4. Who had the item last, on what document, from when? When was it last inventoried, and by whom?
5. What do the custodian and the last user say happened? (Summaries by label.)
6. Is there any indication the loss involved a weapon, ammunition, classified material, or crypto? (Then stop; separate reporting.)
7. What is the deadline the command sets from discovery to initiation? Who is the appointing authority?
