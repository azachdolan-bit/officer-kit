# Memo exemplars, fictional, annotated

Form only. Every number, date, code, and phrase here is invented and is not to be taken into a product; `memo_check.py` fails a draft that lifts a sentence from this file. Read this after the intake is done and the fact list is confirmed, never before, so it teaches shape and supplies no answers. The user's own papers in `Reference/Exemplars/memo/` beat this file.

## Strong: a memorandum for the record (memo.md, built by build_letter.py with "kind": "mfr")

```
# Memo
Kind: MFR   Code: S-3   Date: 12 November 2026
Signer: <MARINE>, Assistant Operations Officer, 1stLt USMC

## Subj
COORDINATION MEETING FOR THE DECEMBER RIFLE RANGE

## Paragraphs
1. On 12 November 2026 at 0900 the assistant operations officer met with the range officer in charge, the battalion gunner, and the four company gunnery sergeants to fix the firing order for the rifle range of 8 to 10 December 2026.
2. Decided: Company A fires 8 December, Companies B and C fire 9 December, Company D and headquarters fire 10 December. The range officer in charge will hold 40 lanes each day. The battalion gunner confirmed 26,400 rounds are on hand against a requirement of 24,200.
3. Action underway: the range order goes to the executive officer for signature by 21 November 2026; each company reports its unqualified count to the S-3 by 24 November 2026. Reason: range control's request cutoff is 28 November 2026.
```

Annotation. The MFR records: who met (by billet), when, what was decided as decisions, what was confirmed as numbers, and what action is now underway and why, which is exactly what MCTP 3-30A says the paper is for ("a record of action underway and reasons for the action"). Nothing argues. The signer block carries name, billet, and grade and service as Appendix D shows. The Subj is a filing phrase in capitals. Three paragraphs; a reader six months later knows what was agreed and by whom.

## Strong: a From-To memorandum (SECNAV M-5216.5 10-2.2, on the OPNAV printed form)

```
# Memo
Kind: From-To memorandum   Date: 21 October 2026
Form: OPNAV 5216/144A

## From
Adjutant (S-1)

## To
Company Commanders

## Subj
LEAVE REQUESTS FOR THE HOLIDAY LEAVE PERIOD

## Paragraphs
1. Leave requests reach the S-1 five working days before the first day of leave. Requests covering the period 18 December 2026 to 4 January 2027 reach the S-1 by 1600 on 1 December 2026.
2. The S-1 returns signed requests to the company offices within two working days and sends the consolidated leave roster to the executive officer every Thursday.
3. A request received after 1 December 2026 is worked in the order received and may not be signed before the leave period begins.

## Signature
<MARINE>
```

Annotation. This is the printed form, so the page above the first paragraph is preprinted and the writer fills DATE, FROM, TO, and SUBJ. The date is the only identification symbol the manual requires here (10-2.3.a and figure 10-2, paragraph 3), so there is no Code line and none is missing. The From and To lines carry titles, which the manual allows alongside names or codes (figure 10-2, paragraph 4). The Signature is the name alone: "The writer signs his or her name without the organizational titles" (figure 10-2, paragraph 6), so no billet and no grade go under it, which is the opposite of the MFR above and of a naval letter. It is going to more than one addressee, which the manual permits ("Memorandum may be directed to one or more addressees"), and it stays inside the activity, which is what this format is for. If the same thing had to reach a via addressee, it would move to the plain-paper memorandum under 10-2.3. Three paragraphs, each one instruction a company office can act on.

## Strong: a business letter (SECNAV M-5216.5 chapter 11)

```
# Memo
Kind: Business letter   SSIC: 1710   Code: Ser S-3/214   Date: October 21, 2026

## Inside Address
Mr. <ADDRESSEE>
Director of Community Relations
<COMPANY NAME>
100 Commerce Street
Jacksonville, NC 28540-1234

## Salutation
Dear Mr. <ADDRESSEE>:

## Subject
SUPPORT TO THE BATTALION FAMILY DAY OF 14 NOVEMBER 2026

## Body
Thank you for your letter of 2 October 2026 offering the loan of two 20 by 40 foot general purpose tents for our family day.

We will hold the event on the battalion parade deck from 1000 to 1400 on 14 November 2026 and expect 620 guests. The two tents cover the seating we had planned for 240 of them. I have enclosed a site diagram showing where your truck can park and the hours the gate is open.

A member of my staff will telephone your office by 31 October 2026 to confirm the delivery window and the return date.

## Close
Sincerely,

## Signature
<MARINE>
Major, U.S. Marine Corps
Executive Officer

## Enclosures
1. Site diagram, battalion parade deck, dated 20 October 2026

## Copy to
Base Community Plans and Liaison Office
```

Annotation, on the four things a Marine gets wrong here. The date is "October 21, 2026", the civilian format chapter 11-2.1.c prescribes, not 21 Oct 26 and not 21 October 2026. There is no Ref line and no Encl line: the earlier letter and the diagram are mentioned in the body as what they are, because 11-2.7 says to refer to them "in the body of the letter only, without calling them references or enclosures", and the enclosure is then listed after the signature under 11-2.10. The close is "Sincerely," and nothing else; 11-2.8 gives one close, so "Very respectfully" and "Respectfully" do not travel here from the personal letter. The signature starts at the centre of the page on the fourth line below "Sincerely," not the fourth line below the text, with the name in capitals and the grade spelled out (11-2.9.a). Two more that follow: the main paragraphs are indented and unnumbered (11-2.6), and there is no From line anywhere, which is why every outgoing copy needs a letterhead (11-2.13). The subject line is in capitals and one line (11-2.5), and it sits below the salutation here rather than replacing it, which 11-2.5.b allows.

## Weak, a business letter written like a naval letter

```
Kind: Business letter   Date: 21 October 2026
```
[The naval letter's date. 11-2.1.c wants October 21, 2026.]

```
## Ref
(a) Your letter of 2 October 2026
```
[A business letter has no Ref block. 11-2.7: mention the earlier letter in the body, by date, without calling it a reference.]

```
## Body
1. Per reference (a), we accept the loan of the tents. Enclosure (1) is the site diagram.
```
[Three errors in one sentence: a numbered main paragraph (11-2.6 "Do not number main paragraphs"), "reference (a)", and "enclosure (1)". The reader is a company that does not know what a reference (a) is.]

```
## Close
Very respectfully,
```
[Not a close this manual gives. 11-2.8 gives "Sincerely," and gives no alternative.]

```
## Signature
<MARINE>
Maj, USMC
```
[11-2.9.a(2): "Military grade (if any) spelled out." Major, and the figures spell the service out as well.]
