# Counseling exemplars, fictional, annotated

Every name and date is invented.

## Event counseling (performance)

```
# Counseling record: Lance Corporal D. K. Brandt

## A. Administrative
Marine: Lance Corporal D. K. Brandt, motor transport operator, Truck Platoon
Counselor: Sergeant M. L. Ives, Platoon Sergeant
Date: 15 July 2026
Type: event counseling
Period covered: 1 June 2026 to 15 July 2026
References: MCO 1500.61; Battalion counseling worksheet

## C. Observations and incidents
- 12 June 2026: duty changeover time of 0745 given in writing on 11 June; arrived 0815; observed by Corporal A. Roe, duty NCO; corrected on the spot.
- 30 June 2026: tasked at 0900 by the platoon sergeant to transport one Marine to the battalion aid station by 1000; did not depart until told a second time by Corporal Roe at 1010; Marine arrived 1040; observed by the platoon sergeant.
- 8 July 2026: weekly vehicle inspection sheet due 1600 Thursday per the posted platoon schedule; not submitted; submitted 9 July after being asked; observed by the platoon sergeant.

## D. Evaluation
Three missed posted times in five weeks after the expectation was given in writing or posted. On 30 June the delay meant a Marine missed a scheduled appointment and the platoon sergeant spent the morning re-tasking. Lance Corporal Brandt's driving and vehicle maintenance in the same period were without deficiency.

## E. Plan for the next period
1. Report to the duty NCO by 0740 on every duty day through 15 August 2026.
2. Submit the weekly inspection sheet by 1500 Thursday, one hour before the posted time, through 15 August 2026.
3. Mentor: Corporal A. Roe, who will review the day's tasks with Lance Corporal Brandt each morning.
4. Feedback: the platoon sergeant meets Lance Corporal Brandt weekly on Fridays at 1300.
5. Return condition: thirty consecutive days with no missed posted time, by 15 August 2026, ends this plan.
6. End state: Lance Corporal Brandt executes posted times and tasking without a second instruction, and the platoon sergeant tasks him as he tasks any other driver.

## F. Marine's comments
(left for the Marine)

## G. Signatures
Marine: ______________________  Date: ________
Counselor: ___________________  Date: ________
```

Annotations. C is three lines, each with a date, the expectation and how it was known, what happened, who saw it, what was done. No trait word. D names the pattern, the cost (a missed appointment, a morning), and one honest strength. E has dated tasks, a mentor by grade and name with a role, a cadence with a day and time, a return condition with a number and a date, and an end state in mission terms. Nothing about why the Marine was late; if the counselor knows a reason that belongs elsewhere, it stays elsewhere.

## Initial counseling (new to the billet)

```
## B. Billet and standards
Billet: Chemical, Biological, Radiological, and Nuclear Defense Specialist, squadron CBRN section. A normal week: two training periods taught or assisted, weekly equipment checks on the section's gear, and one day supporting the flight line.
Standards: report problems to the section chief the day they are found, not at the weekly check; equipment checks are logged the same day; a training period is ready 24 hours before it runs (roster, gear, classroom).
Goals for the period:
1. Gas chamber operator certification complete by 30 September 2026.
2. Teach one full training period unassisted by 31 October 2026.
3. Complete the MarineNet Leading Marines course by 30 November 2026.
Next counseling: 15 October 2026.
```

Annotation: standards are the leader's real expectations, not MOS prerequisites; goals are three, each with a date; the next date is set.

## What the weak version looks like

"LCpl Brandt has a bad attitude and is constantly late. He needs to fix his motivation or he will be separated. He has had personal issues at home which may be the cause." Three failures: a trait instead of incidents; a promise the sergeant cannot keep; family content in a record. The intake produces the version above from the same facts.
