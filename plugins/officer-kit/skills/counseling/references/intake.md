# Counseling intake, by block

One question at a time. Everything recorded is the leader's own observation or attributed to the person who observed it.

## A. Administrative

1. Which type: initial (new to the billet or the leader), follow on (a scheduled check on the last plan), or event (something happened)?
2. Who is the Marine (grade, name, billet) and who is the counselor? What date? Which worksheet does the command use?

## B. Billet and standards (initial counseling)

3. What is the billet, in your words? What does a normal week look like?
4. What standards do you expect that are not obvious from the billet (reporting times, how you want to be told about problems, what "done" means for the section's recurring tasks)?
5. What are the goals for this period, with dates (a course, a qualification, a task finished)?
6. When is the next counseling?

## C. Observations and incidents (event and follow on)

For each incident, four things:

7. What was expected, and how did the Marine know (an order given when, a posted schedule, a standard from the initial counseling)?
8. What happened, on what date?
9. Who observed it? If not you, who, by grade and name?
10. What was done at the time (corrected on the spot, retasked, nothing)?

The standard for an incident line: `<date>: <expectation, and how it was known>; <what happened>; observed by <who>; <action at the time>.` No adjectives. If the user offers a trait ("lazy," "bad attitude"), ask for the incident that showed it and record the incident.

For a follow on: each task from the last plan, with done or not done and the date.

## D. Evaluation

11. What do the incidents add up to, in one sentence? (A pattern of missing posted times; a single serious lapse.)
12. What did it cost the section or the mission, concretely? (A vehicle not moved; a Marine not transported; the platoon sergeant's time.)
13. What does the Marine do well, in one sentence, if that is true? (Optional. Not padding; a fact.)

## E. Plan

14. What tasks for the next period, each with a date?
15. Who is the mentor, by grade and name, and what is their role in the plan?
16. How often will you meet, and on what day?
17. What is the return condition: what, done by when, ends the plan? (Thirty days without a missed reporting time; the qualification completed by a date.)
18. What is the end state, in one sentence, in terms of the section's mission?

## What stays out

Medical, family, financial, disciplinary process, and investigation content, even as the reason. "Referred to the appropriate resource on <date>" is the most a counseling record carries. No promises about promotion, separation, or punishment.
