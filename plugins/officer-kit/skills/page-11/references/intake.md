# Page 11 intake

## 1. Which entry
1. A 6105 counseling entry, a not recommended for promotion entry, or both? Who signs (the commander, or a counselor the commander designated)?
2. Has the legal officer or SJA been consulted? What did they say about wording?

## 2. The deficiency
3. What is the deficiency, as dated facts? Each occurrence with a date. What standard does it violate (an order, a regulation, a posted instruction), by number where one exists?
4. What prior counseling exists on the same deficiency, with dates? (From counseling records; those are the evidence the separation package will need.)

## 3. Corrective action
5. What will the Marine do, observably, that fixes it? By when? How will the counselor know?
6. What assistance does the command actually have for this (a named program, a chaplain, a substance abuse counselor, a financial counselor, a mentor by billet), and how does the Marine reach it?

## 4. Consequences
7. Beyond administrative separation under MCO 1900.16, what other actions may the command take (NJP, adverse fitness report or JEPES marks, not recommended for promotion, loss of privileges)? Only those that are true.

## 5. Opportunity
8. What period is the Marine given, and on what date will the commander re evaluate?

## 6. Not recommended for promotion
9. What month would the Marine otherwise be promoted? (The unit diary entry and the Page 11 are due by the 15th of the month before.) What is the reason, as a fact?

## What stays out
Medical, family, and financial detail; anything from an investigation beyond what the commander directs after SJA review. The entry states the deficiency, not the story behind it.
