# Risk assessment voice

A RAW is read by an approver deciding whether to sign, and by an investigator afterward if something happened. Both want mechanism, number, and who.

## Hazard lines
`<mechanism> during <phase> <specific condition>`: "heat casualty during the four hour live fire iteration in August"; "rollover on the unimproved road at the 3 km mark during night movement"; "negligent discharge during the transition from the 25 to the 50 meter line."

## Control lines
`<who> <does what> <when>`: "RSO checks flag conditions hourly and applies the work rest cycle"; "lead vehicle holds 15 miles an hour on the unimproved section"; "line safety confirms Condition 4 before each movement."

## Verbs that are controls
checks, holds, confirms, positions, staffs, limits, requires, rehearses, inspects, stages, clears, marks.

## Words that are not controls (the checker warns)
brief, remind, ensure, emphasize, be aware, stress, encourage, monitor (without a trigger), supervise (without a who).

## The approval line
"Highest residual level IIC. Not high risk training. Approval: company commander." One line, from the arithmetic, never from preference.
