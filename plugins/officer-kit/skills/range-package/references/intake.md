# Range package intake

1. Which range, which dates, what event, what unit, how many Marines? What is the base's request lead time and checklist? (Override file.)
2. Weapons and ammunition: each DODIC and count. Any pyrotechnics, demolitions, or non standard ammunition?
3. Who is the OIC and the RSO by billet, and when were they certified by the base's range safety course? Who is the corpsman and what is their certification?
4. The course of fire by serial: what fires from where at what, with what limits? What are the control measures (left and right limits, the firing line, the ECP)?
5. Medical: nearest care, route, time; the evacuation vehicle; who calls range control.
6. Communications: primary net, secondary, check times; the cease fire word and signal.
7. Logistics: ammunition draw and turn in, transport, chow, water, sanitation.
8. What did range control return last time, and why?
