# Standard: MCO 3570.1 series (Range Safety, with AR 385-63) and the Range Safety Pocket Guide (Feb 2024)

The order is hosted externally and was not read verbatim here; the MCPEL entry (23 May 2025) is "MCO 3570.1_". The Range Safety Pocket Guide from the Marine Corps Safety Division (February 2024) summarizes what the OIC and RSO do. Read from safety.marines.mil, 5 September 2026.

## Before firing (pocket guide, page 4)
> Read SOP, local range regs, and local safety brief.
Safety brief given; support personnel assigned and briefed; range setup checked against the layout and perimeters; safety check of all weapons.

## During firing (page 4)
PPE and hearing protection; "Follow the course of fire. Do not deviate."; radio checks; continual monitoring of the impact area.

## After firing (page 4)
Unload and clear all weapons; police call and range breakdown; ammunition accountability through shakedown; personnel and equipment accountability.

## Cease fire (page 5)
> A cease-fire must be given verbally and physically by giving the hand-and-arm signal to cease fire.
On cease fire, all weapons go to Condition 4.

## MEDEVAC (page 5)
> MEDEVAC will be handled by the safety corpsman and the OIC or RSO in conjunction with Range Control.

## The common package list (not from the order; the base's list wins)
Range request in the base's form; the risk assessment (MCO 5100.29C Vol 2); the range order or LOI; the OIC and RSO certification (the base's range safety course); the ammunition request; the medical plan with the corpsman's certification; the communications plan; the roster the base requires; the safety brief.

## To extract from the user's copy
MCO 3570.1's OIC and RSO responsibilities by paragraph; surface danger zone rules; the base range control SOP's checklist.
