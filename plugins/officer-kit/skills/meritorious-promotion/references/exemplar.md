# Meritorious promotion recommendation, fictional exemplar, annotated

Every name, unit, order number, and figure is invented.

Subj: MERITORIOUS PROMOTION RECOMMENDATION IN THE CASE OF LANCE CORPORAL R. T. HALE 0000000000/5711 USMC

Ref: (a) WgO 1400.0A

Encl: (1) Meritorious Promotion Data Sheet
      (2) Basic Individual Record
      (3) Basic Training Record
      (4) Awards Summary

1. Per reference (a), Lance Corporal Hale is eligible and recommended for meritorious promotion to corporal.

2. Enclosures (1) through (4) are submitted as required by the reference.

3. Lance Corporal Hale has served as a Chemical, Biological, Radiological, and Nuclear Defense Specialist in Marine Wing Support Squadron 000 since March 2025, and as the section's training noncommissioned officer, a billet normally held by a sergeant, since September 2025. His meritorious accomplishments are as follows:

    a. Lance Corporal Hale is 70 inches and 165 pounds, within standards. He scored a first class 285 on the physical fitness test in January 2026 and a first class 292 on the combat fitness test in October 2025. He is a rifle expert (335) and a pistol expert (365), holds a gray belt, and is a water survival intermediate.

    b. He instructed 14 periods of CBRN defense training totaling 41 hours for 312 Marines across three squadrons, and licensed 25 Marines on the decontamination system, closing a shortfall the readiness inspection had identified. He is certified as a gas chamber operator and a decontamination system operator.

    c. He has not yet had the opportunity to deploy.

    d. In November 2025, two days before the squadron's field exercise, the section's lead instructor was reassigned. Lance Corporal Hale ran the CBRN training for the exercise for 140 Marines over three days as the only instructor present and every scheduled period was delivered.

    e. He has led a four Marine section as the training noncommissioned officer since September 2025 and accounts for $410,000 of CBRN defense equipment with zero discrepancies on the December 2025 inventory. He was selected as the squadron's Marine of the Quarter for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026.

    f. He has completed the Leading Marines distance education program and eight MarineNet courses, including Corporals Course distance education. He has read four books from the Commandant's Professional Reading List (titles in enclosure (1)) and has completed nine college credits toward an associate degree.

    g. He has volunteered 46 hours with the base Toys for Tots campaign and 22 hours with the Single Marine Program, and served on the squadron color guard for six ceremonies.

4. My point of contact is Captain A. B. Example at (000) 000-0000.

Annotations. Paragraph 1 is one sentence and cites reference (a). Paragraph 3 leads with the above grade billet and its date. Every lettered paragraph carries the numbers from `categories.md` for its category: (a) five scores with dates, (b) counts with the shortfall closed, (c) one honest sentence, (d) a dated situation with an outcome rather than "handles stress well," (e) the dollar figure and a selection, (f) counts and named courses, (g) hours by organization. Nothing on the strike list. The EDIPI in the subject line is the one place an identifier belongs, and the checker allows it there only.
