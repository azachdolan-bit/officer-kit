# The board's seven categories, in the order the letter carries them

Observed in approved meritorious promotion and NCO recognition packages at two commands five years apart. Local orders may name them differently or add one; when the user's order lists categories, the order wins and the tool maps these onto it. Ask for facts under each, one at a time. A category with nothing in it stays one sentence.

| Letter | Category | What goes in it | Numbers the board expects |
|---|---|---|---|
| a | Personal appearance, height and weight, physical fitness | Height, weight, within standards; PFT and CFT scores with class and date; rifle and pistol scores with class; MCMAP belt; swim qualification | height and weight; PFT and CFT score and class; rifle and pistol score and class; belt; swim level |
| b | MOS credibility and technical competence | What the Marine does in the MOS and how well: events instructed, hours taught, Marines trained, certifications and licenses held, inspections passed with the score | events; hours; personnel trained; certifications by name; inspection results |
| c | Combat tours and deployments | Deployments with unit, dates, and the billet held; or "has not yet had the opportunity to deploy" | deployments with months; billet on deployment |
| d | Emotional maturity and ability to cope with stress | A concrete situation the Marine handled under pressure and what came of it; not a characterization | one dated situation with an outcome |
| e | Leadership qualities and capability | Billets above grade with dates, Marines led, equipment accounted for with the dollar figure, selections (Marine or NCO of the quarter), collateral duties with results | billet grade gap; count led; dollar value; selections |
| f | Professional growth potential | PME status for the grade and the next grade; MarineNet and MCI courses by name and count; Commandant's reading list titles with count; off duty education with credits or degree progress | course count and names; book count and titles; credits; PME completion dates |
| g | Positive leadership influence on the command | Community service hours by organization; color guard, Single Marine Program, unit events organized; mentorship with counts | hours by organization; events; Marines mentored |

## Asking

For each category: "What do you have under (a)? Give me the numbers you can source." Then climb the ladder from the `award` tool on any vague answer: action, scope, result, comparison, consequence. Stop where the record supports it.

For (d), ask for the situation, not the trait: "When was this Marine under pressure, and what did they do?" A sentence like "handles stress well" is not evidence; a dated incident with an outcome is.

## What never goes in

Medical, family, financial, disciplinary, or investigative content, even favorable. Identifiers other than grade and name in the letter body. The forms the order requires carry the identifiers, filled by admin.
