# Training schedule voice

Event lines are nouns with codes: "Squad attack, dry, 0311-ATK-2001". Instructors are billets: "Squad leader, 2d squad". Prep tasks are verbs with dates: "Draw 1,200 rounds 5.56 from ASP, motor transport chief, 11 August".

Strike: "as required," "TBD" without a gap line, "all hands" without a count, "various" as a place.
