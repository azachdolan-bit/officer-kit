# Standard: MCO 1553.3C, Unit Training Management Program (9 Aug 2023)

Read from marines.mil, 4 September 2026. Paragraph numbers were not captured from the PDF's structure; the user confirms against their copy. Verbatim where marked.

## What the order requires of a unit
- The METL is validated annually, within 45 days of assuming command, or within 15 days of receiving a new mission, and published in MCTIMS.
- The commander publishes training guidance; the unit builds a training plan from it; training schedules are the plan's weekly execution and are submitted to higher headquarters.
- MCTIMS is the system of record: T&R events, rosters, risk assessments, and scores are recorded there.
- Training and Readiness manuals (NAVMC 3500 series) carry the events, their codes, prerequisites, conditions, and standards by MOS and unit type.

## Annual requirements
MCO 1500.63A (10 Apr 2026), Marine Corps Common Military Training Requirements: the annual individual training list. Confirm the current list against the user's copy or MarineNet; the schedule carries them as fixed events when due.

## Risk
Every training event with a hazard carries a risk assessment (MCO 5100.29C Vol 2); high risk training is approved in writing by the first O-5. See `risk-assessment`.
