# Training schedule intake

## 1. Frame
1. Which week, which unit, and what did the commander say the week is for?
2. What does the training plan say this week's events are? What annual requirements come due?
3. When is the schedule due to the S-3 and in what format? (Override file.)

## 2. Fixed events
4. What has higher already put on the calendar? What ranges or classrooms are booked? Medical, dental, admin, pay days?

## 3. Unit events
5. For each event: the T&R code (from the NAVMC 3500 manual), the prerequisite events, the standard.
6. Who instructs, by billet? Are they certified where the T&R requires it?
7. Where? Is it booked? What uniform, gear, ammunition, equipment, transport?

## 4. Risk and packages
8. Which events need a RAW? Is it drafted, signed, or not started? Which need a range package?

## 5. Conflicts
9. Who is in two places at once? Which instructor is double booked? Which event has no place?

## 6. Prep
10. For each event: what must happen before it, who does it by billet, by when?
