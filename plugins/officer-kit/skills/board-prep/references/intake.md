# Board preparation intake

One question at a time. "I do not know" is recorded as a gap, never filled. Ask what this officer's record raises, not what board preparation usually raises. Never put a candidate answer inside the question. The product is the officer's own preparation; nothing in it speaks to what the board will do.

## 1. Frame
1. Which board: selection to what grade, and which component (Active, Reserve, AR, LDO)? The line in the MARADMIN 622/25 paragraph 1 table gives the convening date and the Bd.Corr.Due date; read them from the table, never from memory.
2. Your grade, date of rank, component, and PMOS. Does the date of rank meet the TIG in paragraph 4.a by the convening date?
3. Has the zone MARADMIN for this board been published? If yes, are you in zone, above zone, or below zone by the DORs it states? If no, the Zone line reads "not yet announced".
4. Any separation date within 90 days after the convening date, or an approved retirement date (paragraph 4.b)? Any opt out submitted (paragraph 6)? Any deferment within one year of placement on the ADL (paragraph 5)?

## 2. OMPF and MBS (paragraph 7)
5. On what date did you last view the OMPF and the MBS in O-RMA? Each audit line needs the date it was verified; "verified" without a date is not a verification.
6. Additions or deletions in the last 12 months: what did you expect to see, and is each one there? Awards, orders, PME certificates, education, photographs if any, adverse material and its rebuttal.
7. What is missing or wrong, item by item? For each: what document fixes it, who receives it (MMPB-22 for OMPF documents, MMPB-23 for fitness reports), and by what date it must be received (two weeks before the convening date for OMPF documents, paragraph 7.a; the fitness report timelines of MCO 1610.7B, paragraph 7.c).
8. Civilian education in MCTFS (paragraph 7.e): does it show every degree you hold, as the transcript states it?
9. Contact data in MOL and MCTFS (paragraph 11): MCC, unit, billet, phone, and email current?
10. Any classified fitness report in the record (paragraph 9)? If yes, has MMPB-10 been notified, and when?
11. Reserve component only (paragraph 10): CRCR reviewed on what date, with what discrepancies sent to the I&I or CO; is an RQS (NAVMC 10476) prepared, and will it go under a signed cover letter?

## 3. Fitness reports (paragraph 7.c)
12. List the reporting periods end to end from the MBS. Where does one report's end date not meet the next report's start date? For each gap: how long, is the missing report under or over one year old (a reconstructed original, or certified true copies, paragraph 7.c.1), and what has been submitted to MMPB-23 on what date?
13. Any report in processing status, and any request for an administrative change outstanding (up to 30 days, paragraph 7.c.2)?

## 4. Photo
14. The message says nothing about a photograph. What does the convening MARADMIN for your board or your career counselor say is required? Record what they said and the date; the tool does not fill this.

## 5. PME (MCO 1553.4B enclosure (1))
15. For your grade, which course or courses meet the PME complete criteria in the order (the by grade paragraphs in standard.md)? Which have you completed, on what date, and is each completion on the MBS and its certificate in the OMPF (paragraph 7.d)?
16. If not complete: which sub courses are complete, with documented evidence? The message allows a letter to the board describing progress (paragraph 7.d); do you want one?
17. Any fellowship or funded graduate program that changes which course counts (enclosure (1) career level paragraph 5, intermediate level paragraphs 7 and 8)?

## 6. Reading (ALMAR 024/25)
18. Which titles from the FY26 list have you read, and roughly when? Titles only as the ALMAR prints them, by section. Titles from earlier lists go under Archive. The ALMAR's expectation is at least five titles annually; the log records what was read and does not round up.

## 7. Letter to the board (paragraph 8)
19. Do you want to write to the President of the Board? The message says encouraged, not required. If no, the section says so and stops.
20. If yes: what does the letter need to tell the board that the record does not already show? (PME progress with evidence, a correction that will not post in time, an explanation of a gap.) One paragraph per point, each tied to an enclosure or a record fact.
21. Enclosures: what documents go with it, each listed as an enclosure in the letter (paragraph 8.d)? Any letter from another party goes only as a listed enclosure under your signed cover letter.
22. How will it be submitted: the Board Correspondence Portal (preferred; confirm in the Portal, no separate confirmation comes) or email to the organizational mailbox (subject line as paragraph 8.g requires; the automatic reply is the confirmation)? On what date, against the Bd.Corr.Due date in the table?
23. Signature: ink or CAC digital (paragraph 8.b)? Nothing classified in it (paragraph 8.c)?
24. Is any part of the letter a request for non selection (paragraph 8.h)? If yes, the letter records that the officer is still considered, incurs a failure of selection if not recommended, and may lose separation pay under 10 U.S.C. 1174.

## 8. The reader
25. How does your monitor or career counselor want the correspondence laid out (the MMPB-10 template on the officer promotion homepage)? Record it in `Overrides/board-prep.md`.

## The verification ladder
| Rung | Question |
|---|---|
| Item | What document or entry is being checked? |
| Where | OMPF, MBS, MCTFS, MOL, CRCR? |
| Seen | On what date did you see it there? |
| Gap | What is missing or wrong, exactly? |
| Fix | What document, to whom, received by when? |
Stop at the rung the officer can state as a fact with a date.
