Annotation. The header reads the convening date and the Bd.Corr.Due date from the paragraph 1 table for the board named, and the OMPF document deadline is two weeks before convening as paragraph 7.a says; the checker recomputes nothing else. Every "Yes" in the audit carries the date it was seen; the two "No" items point to the section that carries the fix. The fitness report gap is stated as dates and a day count, the paragraph 7.c.1 rule that applies is named (over one year old, certified true copies), and the fix has a date and a follow up. The Photo section says what the message does not say and records the counselor's statement as a statement. PME is required, then held, then status; the order's own caution that PME cannot be tied to promotion is quoted rather than the officer's opinion about it. The reading log counts what was read against the ALMAR's aim and does not round up. The letter tells the board one thing the record does not show, lists its enclosure, is addressed as paragraph 8.a requires, signed as 8.b requires, and its submission line carries the method, the date, and the due date from the header. Nothing in the file says what the board will do.
