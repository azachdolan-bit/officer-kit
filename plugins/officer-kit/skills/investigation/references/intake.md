# Investigation intake

The order is the handbook's order of work. One question at a time. The tool records the IO's answers; it never supplies a fact.

## 1. The convening order
1. Is there a written convening order? If not, this is a preliminary inquiry; if so, it is enclosure (1). What does it direct the IO to investigate, and what specific questions does it direct the IO to address?
2. What is the due date? (30 days from the order unless it says otherwise.) Any special instructions (litigation report, death, NCIS involvement)? If any of those, stop and go to the SJA.
3. Has the IO consulted the SJA? What did the SJA say about warnings and format?

## 2. The evidence plan
4. What documents exist (orders, schedules, logs, rosters, messages, photographs, maps)? Where are the originals and who holds them?
5. What scenes or objects should the IO see, and when?
6. Who saw what? List witnesses by billet or label with what each is expected to know. Who, if anyone, is suspected of an offense, misconduct, or improper performance? (That witness is interviewed last, after the Article 31(b) warning.)
7. Is anyone injured, or is a claim against the Navy possible? (Then no signed statements; summaries of testimony.)

## 3. Interviews
8. For each witness: what does the convening order need from this person? The tool drafts the question list from the order; the IO asks and records. Which warning applies (31(b), injury statement, Privacy Act)?

## 4. Findings of fact
9. In chronological order, what happened? Each fact one line, with the person by billet or label, the time, the place, and the enclosure that proves it. If a fact has no enclosure, it is not yet a finding; what would prove it?
10. Where does the evidence conflict, and which is more reliable, and why? (This goes in the preliminary statement.)

## 5. Opinions
11. From the findings, what does the IO conclude about cause, responsibility, and accountability? Each opinion cites the findings it rests on. What did the CA direct the IO to opine on?

## 6. Recommendations
12. From the opinions, what should the CA do: corrective, administrative, disciplinary (forum and charges, not "disciplinary action")? Each cites the opinions it rests on.

## 7. The preliminary statement
13. Was all reasonably available evidence collected, or is some forthcoming? Any delays, difficulties, extensions (verbal ones too)? Where are the originals kept and who is the custodian?
