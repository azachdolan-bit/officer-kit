# Standard: JAGINST 5800.7G (JAGMAN) Chapter II, and the Naval Justice School JAGMAN Investigations Handbook (24 Oct 2024)

Read from the NJS handbook on the Navy JAG site, 5 September 2026. Page references are the handbook's. The JAGMAN itself governs; the handbook is the working guide. Read the user's copy of either when it is in Reference; the SJA's word beats both.

## Preliminary inquiry (handbook page II-1)
> quick and informal analytical tool ... quick first look at an incident ... not meant to provide all answers except in the simplest cases.
> should not take longer than three working days. If more time is required, it generally means the inquiry officer is attempting to do too much.
> need not be in writing, but some form of limited documentation is advisable.
The convening authority may conduct it personally or appoint a member of the command; there are no requirements or restrictions governing how it is accomplished. Sample documentation at JAGMAN Appendix A-2-c.

## Appointment (page III-3)
The investigating officer is appointed in writing by the convening authority; begins work immediately; must consult with the cognizant staff judge advocate; must consult the Office of General Counsel if civilians are involved.

## Timeline (page III-1)
> normally 30 days from the date of the convening order.
The CA may grant extensions as needed; extensions need not be in writing but must be noted in the preliminary statement of the final report.

## The report's parts, in order (page III-7)
Preliminary statement; findings of fact; opinions; recommendations; signature; enclosures.

## Preliminary statement (page III-7)
Must state that all reasonably available evidence was collected or is forthcoming; the nature of the investigation; any delays or difficulties encountered; an explanation of conflicting evidence and reliability assessments; extension requests and their status; the location and safekeeping of original evidence with custodian contact information. Mark the classification; omit classified information unless essential.

## Findings of fact (page III-8)
Specific as to persons, times, places, and events; chronological or logical order; each fact a separate finding.
> Each FoF, reference in order the enclosures to the report that support the FoF.
Each finding supported by at least one enclosure. Findings read together tell the complete story without the enclosures. Facts are distinguished from opinions and recommendations.

## Opinions (page III-8)
> reasonable evaluations, inferences, or conclusions based on the facts found.
Each opinion references the supporting findings of fact; opinions are value judgments, distinct from facts; they address responsibility and accountability and any area the convening authority directed.

## Recommendations (page III-9)
Proposals based on opinions; logical and consistent with the findings and opinions; corrective, disciplinary, or administrative action.
> be specific, don't just recommend "disciplinary action," suggest a forum and charges.
If a punitive letter of reprimand is recommended, enclose a draft; non punitive letters go under separate cover.

## Enclosures (page III-9, III-7)
> The first enclosure is always the convening order.
All evidence in logical order tracking with the findings; each statement, affidavit, transcript, summary of testimony, photograph, map, chart, document, or other exhibit a separate enclosure; reproduced documents certified true copies; photographs marked per JAGMAN 0207(b)(4); enclosures listed in citation order; unused enclosures deleted. Practice tip: do not number enclosures while drafting.
Prohibited as enclosures: NCIS reports of investigation, aircraft mishap reports, Inspector General reports, polygraph examinations, medical quality assurance investigations.

## Warnings before interviews (pages III-4 to III-5)
Article 31(b), UCMJ: required for any military witness suspected of an offense, misconduct, or improper performance of duty; sample at JAGMAN Appendix A-1-o.
> Ordinarily, an investigation should collect relevant information from all other sources before interviewing a suspect.
Injury or disease statement: advise each witness, before signing any statement relating to the origin, incident, or aggravation of any disease or injury they have suffered, of their right not to sign such a statement (JAGMAN 0212(c), Appendix A-1-j).
> No signed witness statements should be taken in any incident involving personal injury or a potential claim against the DON.
Privacy Act statement: required when personal information is taken from a witness (JAGMAN 0207(e), Appendix A-2-h).

## Out of the tool's scope
Death cases (20 days; litigation report rules); litigation report investigations (JA supervision); anything involving NCIS beyond what the CA states; classified matters. The SJA first.
