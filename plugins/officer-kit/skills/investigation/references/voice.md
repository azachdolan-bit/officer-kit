# Investigation voice

Findings: past tense, one fact, specific. "At approximately 1410 on 12 August 2026, <WITNESS 2>, the platoon sergeant, moved the relay vehicle from the high ground to the entry control point. [Encl (3), (5)]"

Opinions: "That the 40 minute loss of the company net was caused by the movement of the relay vehicle before serial 3 was complete. [FF 4, 5, 6]"

Recommendations: "That the company standing operating procedure for range operations be amended to require the relay vehicle to remain in position until endex. [Op 1]"

Words that turn a finding into an opinion (the checker warns): probably, likely, appears, seems, believe, should have, negligent, careless, failed to (when the standard is not cited), reckless.

Labels: `<SUBJECT>`, `<WITNESS 1>`, `<WITNESS 2>`, billets. Never a name in the body.
