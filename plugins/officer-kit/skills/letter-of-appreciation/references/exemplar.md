# Letter of appreciation: exemplar and intake

Fictional throughout. The shape is drawn from a signed letter from a wing level director to a sergeant that went into his record.

## Exemplar

Subj: LETTER OF APPRECIATION

1. On 14 and 15 May 2026 you served as the range safety officer for the wing's motorcycle mentorship ride, a two day event that trained 38 Marines from six squadrons in group riding, hazard recognition, and mentorship of new riders.

2. Every one of the 38 completed both days, and 11 of them are now registered mentors in their squadrons' programs, which raises the wing's mentor count by a third. The wing has had no motorcycle mishap involving a mentored rider in the eighteen months the program has run, and events like this one are the reason.

3. You planned the route, briefed the safety plan each morning, and rode as the trail safety vehicle for both days on your own time. Please accept my sincere thanks for your part in the wing's program. A copy of this letter will be placed in your official record.

Annotations. Paragraph 1: dates, event, count, source of the count (six squadrons). Paragraph 2: the outcome in numbers (38 completed, 11 mentors, a third) and the effect on the program. Paragraph 3: the specific role in three verbs, the thanks once, and the line that makes it matter to the Marine. Three paragraphs, under 200 words, no word from the strike list.

## Intake

1. Who is being thanked (grade, name, unit) and who signs? Does it go in the record? If so, the letter goes to the individual via their commanding officer, and the last line says so.
2. What was the event, where, and on what dates?
3. How many people, what completed, what changed, and for what program or unit? One or two numbers.
4. What exactly did this person do that made it possible? Three verbs at most.
5. Does the signer want a POC line? (Usually not on a letter of appreciation.)

If the answers describe a period of service rather than an event, or an outcome that the awards manual would recognize, say so and offer the `award` tool. Then write the letter if that is still what the user wants.
