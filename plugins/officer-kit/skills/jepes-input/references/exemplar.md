# JEPES input exemplars, fictional, annotated

Form only. Every number, date, count, and phrase here is invented and is not to be taken into a product; `jepes_input_check.py` fails a draft that lifts a sentence from this file. Read this after the intake is done and the fact list is confirmed, never before, so it teaches shape and supplies no answers. The user's own approved products in `Reference/Exemplars/jepes-input/` beat this file.

## Strong: First Line Supervisor, semiannual occasion, marks in three bands

```
# JEPES command input
Marine: <MARINE>, Lance Corporal, 0311, rifleman   Prepared by: First Line Supervisor (squad leader)   Approver: battalion commanding officer
Occasion: SA (Semi-Annual)   From: 1 February 2026   To: 31 July 2026   Submitted: 21 July 2026   Supervision began: 6 October 2025   Initial counseling: 23 October 2025
References: MCO 1616.1 enclosure (1) chapter 1 paragraphs 3.b and 6, chapter 2 paragraphs 2 and 3

## Individual Character
Mark: 2.7 (Meets Expectations)
Facts: Returned a wallet with 340 dollars found on the range road to the armory on 12 April 2026 and reported it through the squad leader; no counseling in the period. The fact moves the mark from 2.5 to 2.7 and no further; no commendatory material in the period.

## MOS and/or Mission Accomplishment
Mark: 3.5 (Exceeds Expectations)
Facts: Qualified expert on the annual rifle range 9 March 2026 with a score that placed 3rd of the 41 lance corporals in the company. Carried the squad's automatic rifle through the 4 to 17 May 2026 field exercise with no weapon stoppage across 6 live fire attacks, against a platoon average of 2 stoppages per gunner. No formal commendatory material in the period, so the mark stays below 4.1.

## Leadership
Mark: 2.5 (Meets Expectations)
Facts: Led the fire team for 11 days from 8 to 18 June 2026 while the team leader was at a course; the team met every formation and completed the 15 June 2026 gear inspection with 2 discrepancies, the platoon's median. Nothing in the period moves the mark from 2.5.

## Promotion recommendation
Recommended for promotion. No adverse material in the period.

## Debrief
Planned for 4 August 2026 by the First Line Supervisor once the Approver approves the occasion. Focus for next period: hold the fire team billet for a full training cycle and complete the MCMAP gray belt course scheduled 14 September 2026.
```

Annotation. Every mark has a dated fact inside 1 February to 31 July 2026 under it, and each justification says why the mark stops where it does: the character fact moves 2.5 to 2.7; the MOS facts are counts with a comparison (3rd of 41; no stoppages against a platoon average of 2) and the last sentence names the order's reason the mark is not Exceptional; the leadership mark stays at the order's starting point and says so with a fact rather than a blank. The submission date is inside the 45 day window before 31 July. The header carries the supervision start and the initial counseling date so the chain can see the FLS met the 30 day requirement. The debrief is a date and a focus, not praise.

## Strong: the initial counseling the order requires within 30 days

```
# JEPES initial counseling
Marine: <MARINE>, Private First Class, 0621, radio operator   Prepared by: First Line Supervisor (section leader)   Approver: battalion commanding officer
Occasion: Initial counseling   Supervision began: 3 August 2026   Due: 2 September 2026   Conducted: 17 August 2026
References: MCO 1616.1 enclosure (1) chapter 1 paragraph 6.b, chapter 2 paragraph 1.b(1)

## Billet description
Radio operator, communications section, headquarters platoon. Operates and maintains 2 manpack radios and the section's 1 vehicle mounted radio; keeps the section's 3 loadsets current.

## Role in the unit
Provides the company commander's net with the forward radio when the company is in the field; one of 4 operators in the section.

## Responsibilities
Daily communications check on the company net by 0730; weekly preventive maintenance on the assigned radios logged on the section's card; loads and verifies the current loadset within 24 hours of each key change.

## Performance expectations
Zero missed daily checks in a month; preventive maintenance logged every week; a passed loadset verification on the first attempt at each key change; PFT and CFT above the section's median at the next test; MCMAP tan belt complete by 31 January 2027.

## Objective scores validated
Rifle, PFT, CFT, MCMAP, informal PME, and self education reviewed with the Marine on 17 August 2026 against MCTFS; the CFT score from 22 May 2026 is not posted and the Marine will bring the scoresheet to the training NCO by 24 August 2026.
```

Annotation. The four elements the order names are each a section (billet description, role in the unit, responsibilities, performance expectations), the expectations are measurable (zero, every week, first attempt, the median, a date), the conducted date is inside the 30 days, and the objective score validation the order pairs with the counseling is recorded with the corrective action and its date. Nothing here is a mark.

## Weak, annotated line by line

```
## Leadership
Mark: 4.4 (Exceptional)
Facts: A natural leader who always steps up and is the go to Marine in the platoon.
```
[No date, no fact, three strike phrases. The order requires a mark at 4.1 or above to be supported by formal commendatory material visible in JEPES and a directed comment; none is named. The checker fails this twice: no dated fact, and Exceptional without commendatory material.]

```
Occasion: SA (Semi-Annual)   From: 1 January 2026   To: 30 June 2026
```
[Not a semiannual period the order recognizes. SA runs 1 February to 31 July or 1 August to 31 January; the checker's occasion table fails the TO date.]

```
Mark: 3.8 (Meets Expectations)
```
[3.8 is Exceeds Expectations. The band name must match the number, or the reader cannot tell which the preparer meant.]

```
Facts: Ran the platoon's supply account since January 2025 and has done well since then.
```
[Outside the period, and "since then" has no date. Command input is based on accomplishments during the reporting period.]

```
Mark: 0.6 (Below Expectations)
Facts: Struggled in the shop this period.
```
[The order places 0.1 to 0.9 on counseling, documented or informal, that occurred during the period, and requires a directed comment. No counseling date, no comment named.]

```
Sergeant <MARINE> is a solid corporal and will pick up sergeant next cycle.
```
[Two failures. The grade in the header decides the tool: a Sergeant is not evaluated under this order. "Will pick up" is a promise about the cutting score.]

```
Submitted: 2 May 2026   To: 31 July 2026
```
[More than 45 days before the end of the period. The FLS may draft anytime but may not submit recommended marks earlier than 45 days out.]
