The order is the Marine Corps processing order. It says of itself (enclosure (1) paragraph 1.c) that SECNAVINST 1650.1 "is the definitive document for all matters pertaining to Naval Service decorations, medals, and awards"; that manual is not in the library (see the last section). For the Meritorious Mast and the Certificate of Commendation the order's own text is the governing text on who may award, when a Meritorious Mast is not conducted, the forms, and the routing.

## The order covers the two products (basic order paragraph 3)
> 3. Information. Information concerning the administration of awards for Marine Corps personnel and Marine Corps organizations can be found in enclosures (1) through (5). Awards encompass all personal, unit, campaign, and service awards (including those given by foreign governments), battle streamers, certificates of commendation, meritorious masts, letters of appreciation, and other forms of public recognition merited by Marines and Marine Corps units.

## Enclosure (1), Awards Administration

### Paragraph 1.c, 1.e, 1.f, the manuals the order points to
> c. SECNAVINST 1650.1 (Navy and Marine Corps Awards Manual) sets forth eligibility requirements, administrative procedures for recommending awards (including sample citations, time limitations, and precedence of awards), policies concerning the presentation of awards and delegation of awarding authority. This Manual is directive in nature on Marine Corps units and is the definitive document for all matters pertaining to Naval Service decorations, medals, and awards. This Manual is supplemented by periodic MARADMIN's that provide amplification to or modification of policies pertaining to Naval Service Awards.
> [...]
> e. MCO P1070.12 (IRAM) contains instructions for recording issuance of awards in service records.
> f. MCO P1610.7 (PES SYSTEM) gives guidance on the disposition of commendatory correspondence.

### Paragraph 1.l(5), who holds Navy and Marine Corps Achievement Medal authority (this sets who may issue a Certificate of Commendation under enclosure (2) paragraph 8.f)
> (5) Commanding officers and other officers assigned as Special Courts Martial Convening Authorities may award the Navy and Marine Corps Achievement Medal (NA) without regard to a quota system. The NA may only be awarded to junior officers and enlisted personnel (LCDR/Maj or below).

### Paragraph 2, policy
> 2. Policy for Personal Decorations and Awards. The basic awards policy is detailed in the reference. Awards are an important aspect of command responsibility at all levels. Prompt and judicious recognition of an individual's achievement or service is a vital factor of morale. Commanders will:
> a. Examine specific actions and outstanding performance then prepare and approve an award or submit recommendations for appropriate awards up the chain of command for decision.
> b. Limit decorations to those personnel whose performance of duty is exceptional and clearly recognized by superiors and contemporaries alike.

### Paragraph 5.a(1) and (2), presentation
> (1) Presentation of personal awards will be made to all Marines as soon as practical and with appropriate ceremony and publicity.
> (2) The Marine Corps' policy of presenting decorations at appropriate ceremonies aboard military installations is outlined in NAVMC 2691. Presentations will be made in the presence of Marines and will not be unduly delayed for the purpose of gathering favorable publicity for the Marine Corps.

### Paragraph 9.a and 9.b, the Certificate of Commendation forms and where the copies go
> 9. Certificates and Presentation Folders
> a. Certificates of Commendation with Record File Copy (NAVMC 10631 and NAVMC 10631A). Delegated authorities will requisition the certificates through the supply system. The certificates come in sets consisting of an original (NAVMC 10631) for issue to the individual concerned and a green copy (NAVMC 10631A) on which the individual's social security number is to be added before forwarding to CMC (MMSB) for entering into the OMPF. DO NOT FORWARD APPROVED, COMMAND LEVEL CERTCOMS TO CMC (MMMA).
> b. Instructions for issuance of the certificates are in paragraph 5. Originals or record copies of the certificate are not to be used for typing rough drafts of the citation.

### Paragraph 9.f, the presentation folder
> f. Presentation Folder. A red plastic folder with the Marine Corps emblem embossed in gold should be used by commands for any type of award presentation.
> (1) Commanders may order the red plastic folder through the supply system using NSN 7510-01-056-1927.

## Enclosure (2), Administrative Procedures for Submission of Recommendations for Personal Decorations and Other Special Recognition

### Paragraph 1, the recognition ladder
> 1. A review of the current awards system revealed that provisions for recognizing exceptional performance of duty are generally being used to the desired extent. Recognizing Marines for their superior performance above the required proficiency and conduct and fitness report markings will significantly benefit the Marine Corps as well as the Marine recommended. In making judgments regarding the level of special recognition, the magnitude of the achievement or service and the level of responsibility of the Marine are pertinent factors that must be considered. Meritorious Masts and Letters of Appreciation also provide ways by which special acknowledgment of a Marine's performance can be recognized. In those cases where none of the above is considered appropriate, field Certificates of Commendation or CMC Certificates of Commendation should be considered. Personal decorations should be limited to those cases in which the performance of duty was so exceptionally superior that its significance and contribution to the Marine Corps are clearly recognized by superiors and contemporaries alike.

(The ladder the paragraph lays out, bottom to top: proficiency and conduct marks and fitness report markings; Meritorious Mast or Letter of Appreciation; field or CMC Certificate of Commendation; personal decoration. The two factors it names for the level are the magnitude of the achievement or service and the level of responsibility of the Marine.)

### Paragraph 6, level of award
> 6. Level of Award. The awards considered appropriate for noncombatant service by personnel falling under the purview of this Order are set forth in SecNavinst 1650.1. Quality and extent of meritorious service performed by the individual and level of responsibility, not the grade of the individual, should be the primary factors in making a determination as to the level of the award proposed.

### Paragraph 8.e(5) and (6), the certificate rules the order does set, and for which certificates
> (5) The signature line of the certificate will include name, grade, official title of the awarding official, and unit identification.
> (6) Samples of the certificates for the NC and NA are at enclosure (4). The certificates will be typed in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS, 9 point, Times New Roman font, Landscape and are limited to 1200 characters (nine lines).

(These two subparagraphs sit under 8.e, Delegated Awards, and (6) names the NC and NA certificates. The order sets no character limit, line count, font, or capitalization rule for the text of a Certificate of Commendation or a Meritorious Mast. The award tool's extract notes the 1200 and 1250 character figures the order gives for the NA and NC; neither applies here by the order's terms.)

### Paragraph 8.f, Certificates of Commendation
> f. Certificates of Commendation (NAVMC 10631)
> (1) Certificates of Commendation may be issued by any general officer. Commanders who have delegated authority to approve the NA may also issue Certificates of Commendation.
> (2) All other officers desiring to make recommendations for Certificates of Commendation shall submit them to the general officer or Commander having NA authority for approval and issue. These awards will not be submitted via the HQMC APS and do not require a 1650 (EF). Do not forward a copy of the approved Certificate of Commendation to CMC (MMMA). A copy, however, should be forwarded to CMC (MMSB) for inclusion into the Marine's OMPF.
> (3) Certificates of Commendation may be issued to civilians who are serving with the Marine Corps. Certificates of Commendation may be presented to other civilians as deemed appropriate to recognize those who have made significant contributions to the Marine Corps.
> (4) Instructions for procurement of Certificates of Commendation are provided in enclosure (1), paragraph 9.
> (5) In those cases in which a CMC Certificate of Commendation is warranted, a recommendation shall be submitted via the chain of command to CMC (MMMA), on the HQMC APS, with a "Summary of Action" and proposed citation.

### Paragraph 8.g, Meritorious Mast
> g. Meritorious Mast. Whenever the performance of an enlisted Marine is considered noteworthy or commendable beyond the usual requirements of duty or the Marine demonstrates exceptional industry, judgment, or initiative, the senior person who has observed the Marine's performance shall make a report of this to the Marine's Commander (battalion or equivalent echelon). These reports shall be given careful consideration and, if appropriate, a Meritorious Mast held.
> (1) A Meritorious Mast shall not be conducted when the service or performance of the Marine is recognized through the awarding of a Letter of Appreciation, Certificate of Commendation, or a personal decoration.
> (2) Commanders will use the Meritorious Mast (NAVMC 10935) form as a uniform means of recognizing efforts that warrant recognition. The form, which is a two-part set, is available through the normal supply channels (NSN 0109 LF-064-6500). Do not forward copies of approved Meritorious Mast's to CMC (MMMA). A copy, however, should be forwarded to CMC (MMSB) for inclusion into the Marines OMPF.

### Paragraph 8.h, Letter of Appreciation
> h. Letter of Appreciation. Any officer senior to a Marine whose performance is considered noteworthy or commendable beyond the usual requirements of duty may issue a Letter of Appreciation. Do not forward copies of Letters of Appreciation to CMC (MMMA) or to CMC (MMSB). A copy will not be entered into the Marine's OMPF.

### Paragraph 8.i, commendatory correspondence rewritten into one of the three
> (1) When commendatory correspondence is received at CMC (MMMA), it will be forwarded directly to the commanding officer of the Marine concerned.
> (2) The commanding officer receiving commendatory correspondence regarding a Marine shall take appropriate action to recognize that individual's achievements. If the commanding officer feels the achievements stated in the commendatory correspondence are enough to rewrite the achievement into a Meritorious Mast, Certificate of Commendation, or personal award recommendation, it will be processed as outlined in this Order and filed in the Marine's OMPF.

## Enclosure (3), paragraph 2.g, the CMC Certificate of Commendation for a unit (not this tool's product)
> If a Commander feels that the award of a MU is not justified, a recommendation of the SecNav Letter of Commendation or CMC Certificate of Commendation is appropriate.

## What the order settles for this tool
- Who awards. Meritorious Mast: the Marine's Commander (battalion or equivalent echelon), on a report from the senior person who observed the performance (8.g). Certificate of Commendation: any general officer, or a commander with delegated NA authority, which enclosure (1) paragraph 1.l(5) gives to commanding officers and other officers assigned as Special Courts Martial Convening Authorities (8.f(1)); any other officer submits the recommendation to that officer (8.f(2)). Letter of Appreciation: any officer senior to the Marine (8.h).
- Who is eligible. Meritorious Mast: an enlisted Marine (8.g). Certificate of Commendation: Marines, and civilians serving with or contributing to the Marine Corps (8.f(3)).
- When a Meritorious Mast is not held. When the same service or performance is recognized by a Letter of Appreciation, a Certificate of Commendation, or a personal decoration (8.g(1)). One recognition per service.
- The forms. Meritorious Mast on NAVMC 10935, a two part set (8.g(2)). Certificate of Commendation on NAVMC 10631 (original to the Marine) with the green record copy NAVMC 10631A, on which the social security number is added before forwarding (enclosure (1) 9.a). Rough drafts are not typed on originals or record copies (enclosure (1) 9.b).
- Routing. Both: a copy to CMC (MMSB) for the OMPF; nothing to CMC (MMMA). The Certificate of Commendation is not submitted through the HQMC APS and needs no 1650 (EF) (8.f(2)). The Letter of Appreciation goes to neither MMSB nor MMMA and is not entered in the OMPF (8.h). A CMC Certificate of Commendation is the exception: it goes via the chain of command to CMC (MMMA) on the HQMC APS with a Summary of Action and proposed citation (8.f(5)).
- Format. None set by the order for the text of either product. The all capitals, 9 point, landscape, 1200 character rule is for the NC and NA certificates (8.e(6)). The signature line rule at 8.e(5) (name, grade, official title, unit identification) is written for delegated award certificates; it is the only signature line rule in the order and the tool follows it for the certificate's signature block as the nearest rule, marked as such.
- Presentation. As soon as practical, with ceremony, in the presence of Marines (enclosure (1) 5.a); the red presentation folder for any award presentation (enclosure (1) 9.f).

## Not in the library
- SECNAV M-1650.1 (Navy and Marine Corps Awards Manual), which the order calls the definitive document. It would settle the criteria language for each recognition and whether the current manual changes the Meritorious Mast or Certificate of Commendation procedures above. The award tool's extract (`award/references/mco-1650-19j-extract.md`) notes that the manual's 2019 edition supersedes the order's NA and NC citation format; nothing in that extract touches these two products.
- MARADMIN 024/22 (iAPS). It would settle whether a Certificate of Commendation or a Meritorious Mast is now recorded through the Improved Awards Processing System rather than by the paper NAVMC 10631A green copy and the paper NAVMC 10935 copy to CMC (MMSB) described above. The routing in this tool is the order's; the user's awards clerk or `Overrides/meritorious-mast.md` records the current practice.
- NAVMC 10935 and NAVMC 10631/10631A themselves. The order names them and the NSN; the block layout and any field limits are on the forms, which are not in the library. The text this tool drafts is the narrative the commander signs; the admin section fits it to the form.
- MCO P1070.12 (IRAM) and MCO P1610.7 (PES), which the order points to for recording the award in the service record and for the disposition of commendatory correspondence.
