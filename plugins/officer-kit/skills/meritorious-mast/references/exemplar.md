# Meritorious Mast and Certificate of Commendation exemplars, fictional, annotated

## Strong: Meritorious Mast, commander at battalion echelon

```
# Meritorious Mast
Marine: <MARINE>, Lance Corporal, supply warehouse clerk   Unit: headquarters company, infantry battalion   Awarding officer: Commanding Officer, infantry battalion   Date: 14 September 2026
Reported by: Company Commander, headquarters company, who observed the performance
References: MCO 1650.19J w/Ch 1 enclosure (2) paragraph 8.g; NAVMC 10935

## Level check
Recognition: Meritorious Mast
The facts are beyond the usual requirements of the billet (a clerk found and fixed a warehouse wide error the section had missed for two cycles) and show initiative (the Marine was not tasked). They are a single event inside the section, at the Marine's own grade and billet, with no effect outside the battalion; the order's two factors for a higher level, magnitude and level of responsibility (enclosure (2) paragraph 1), are not present at the Certificate of Commendation step. No Letter of Appreciation, Certificate of Commendation, or personal decoration recognizes or is pending for this service, so 8.g(1) does not bar the Mast: "A Meritorious Mast shall not be conducted when the service or performance of the Marine is recognized through the awarding of a Letter of Appreciation, Certificate of Commendation, or a personal decoration."

## Facts
- 4 August 2026 to 8 August 2026: during the semiannual inventory of the battalion supply warehouse, <MARINE> found 37 line items posted to the wrong account in the property system.
- The 37 items had been miscounted through the two previous inventories (February 2026, August 2025) without detection.
- <MARINE> corrected the 37 records and reconciled the account before the inventory closed on 8 August 2026; the section's inventory closed with 0 discrepancies against 9 at the February 2026 inventory.
- <MARINE> was not assigned to the reconciliation; the supply chief confirmed the Marine took it on after finding the first error.

## Text
Lance Corporal <MARINE> is commended for initiative during the battalion supply warehouse inventory from 4 to 8 August 2026. While counting an assigned section of the warehouse, Lance Corporal <MARINE> found 37 line items posted to the wrong account, an error that had passed through the two previous inventories undetected. Without being tasked, the Marine corrected all 37 records and reconciled the account before the inventory closed on 8 August 2026. The section closed the inventory with 0 discrepancies, against 9 at the inventory in February 2026. Lance Corporal <MARINE>'s attention and initiative gave the battalion an accurate property account and spared the section a third cycle of the same error.

## Routing
- Report: the company commander, who observed the performance, reported it to the battalion commander (enclosure (2) paragraph 8.g); the commander decided to hold the Mast.
- Form: NAVMC 10935, two part set; the text above goes on the form. Signed by the Commanding Officer, infantry battalion (battalion echelon, 8.g).
- Copies: one copy to CMC (MMSB) for the OMPF (8.g(2)). Do not forward a copy to CMC (MMMA) (8.g(2)). Not submitted through the HQMC APS; no 1650 (EF).
- Presentation: read at the company formation, in the presence of Marines (enclosure (1) 5.a), in the red presentation folder (enclosure (1) 9.f).
- Record: awards clerk enters it in the service record per MCO P1070.12 (enclosure (1) 1.e). Current electronic routing, if the command uses one, per Overrides/meritorious-mast.md.
```

Annotation. The Level check names one recognition and says why the facts stop there in the order's own two factors, then quotes 8.g(1) and says it does not apply because nothing else recognizes this service. The Facts are dated and counted; every number in the Text (4, 8, 37, 0, 9, 2026, February) appears in the Facts, so the Text proves nothing the Facts do not. The Text names the initiative (found, corrected without being tasked) and the consequence (an accurate account) without the adjective. The Routing carries what the order requires: the observer's report, the form, the commander at battalion echelon, the MMSB copy, and the MMMA prohibition. The unit is an echelon, not a name.

## Strong: Certificate of Commendation, commander with NA authority

```
# Certificate of Commendation
Marine: <MARINE>, Sergeant, communications chief   Unit: artillery battery, artillery battalion   Awarding officer: Commanding Officer, artillery regiment (Special Courts Martial Convening Authority, NA authority)   Date: 21 September 2026
Recommended by: Commanding Officer, artillery battalion
References: MCO 1650.19J w/Ch 1 enclosure (2) paragraph 8.f; enclosure (1) paragraphs 1.l(5) and 9.a; NAVMC 10631 and NAVMC 10631A

## Level check
Recognition: Certificate of Commendation
The facts exceed a Meritorious Mast on both of the order's factors (enclosure (2) paragraph 1): magnitude, because the effect reached 6 batteries across the regiment for a 3 month period, and level of responsibility, because a sergeant carried a staff sergeant's billet for the period. They do not reach a personal decoration: the work was within the communications field, was not recognized outside the regiment, and the regiment's comparison is to its own prior quarter, not to the Marine Corps. Because a Certificate of Commendation recognizes this service, a Meritorious Mast is not held for it: "A Meritorious Mast shall not be conducted when the service or performance of the Marine is recognized through the awarding of a Letter of Appreciation, Certificate of Commendation, or a personal decoration" (8.g(1)).

## Facts
- 1 May 2026 to 31 July 2026: <MARINE> served as battery communications chief, a staff sergeant billet, while the billet was gapped.
- During the period <MARINE> rewrote the battery's radio maintenance checklist; the battalion adopted it on 12 June 2026 and the regiment issued it to all 6 batteries on 30 June 2026.
- Radio readiness in the battery rose from 71 percent on 1 May 2026 to 94 percent on 31 July 2026; the regiment's average for the same period rose from 78 to 86 percent.
- <MARINE> trained 22 radio operators from 4 batteries on the checklist in 3 sessions in June and July 2026.

## Text
For commendable performance of duty as communications chief of an artillery battery from 1 May 2026 to 31 July 2026. Filling a staff sergeant's billet while it was gapped, Sergeant <MARINE> rewrote the battery's radio maintenance checklist, which the battalion adopted on 12 June 2026 and the regiment issued to all 6 of its batteries on 30 June 2026. Under the checklist the battery's radio readiness rose from 71 percent to 94 percent over the period, while the regiment's average rose from 78 to 86 percent. Sergeant <MARINE> trained 22 radio operators from 4 batteries in 3 sessions. The regiment's batteries now maintain their radios to one standard written by this Marine.

## Routing
- Signature line: <name added on the user's computer>, Colonel, Commanding Officer, artillery regiment (enclosure (2) paragraph 8.e(5): name, grade, official title, unit identification).
- Recommended by the battalion commander to the regimental commander, who holds NA authority as a Special Courts Martial Convening Authority (enclosure (1) 1.l(5)) and may issue Certificates of Commendation (8.f(1)); the battalion commander does not issue it (8.f(2)).
- Not submitted via the HQMC APS; no 1650 (EF) required (8.f(2)).
- Form: NAVMC 10631 original to the Marine; NAVMC 10631A green copy, social security number added on the user's computer before forwarding, to CMC (MMSB) for the OMPF (enclosure (1) 9.a). Do not forward a copy to CMC (MMMA) (8.f(2); enclosure (1) 9.a). Rough drafts are not typed on the original or the record copy (enclosure (1) 9.b).
- Presentation: at the battery formation in the presence of Marines (enclosure (1) 5.a), red presentation folder (enclosure (1) 9.f).
- Current electronic routing, if the command uses one, per Overrides/meritorious-mast.md.
```

Annotation. The Level check climbs the ladder in both directions: why more than a Mast (both factors named with the fact that shows each) and why less than a decoration. Every number in the Text is in the Facts, including the comparison (78 to 86). The awarding officer is identified by billet and by the authority that lets that billet issue the certificate, and the battalion commander is placed as recommender, which is what 8.f(2) requires. The Routing says what does not happen (no APS, no MMMA) as well as what does.
