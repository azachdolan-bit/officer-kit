# Meritorious Mast and Certificate of Commendation intake

One question at a time. "I do not know" is recorded as a gap, never filled. Ask what this Marine's performance raises, not what recognitions usually raise. Never put a candidate answer inside the question.

## 1. Frame
1. Who observed the performance, by billet? (For a Meritorious Mast the order has that person report it to the Marine's commander, battalion or equivalent echelon, who decides whether to hold it; enclosure (2) paragraph 8.g.)
2. Who will sign, by billet, and at what echelon? A Meritorious Mast is the commander's at battalion or equivalent. A Certificate of Commendation is a general officer's or a commander's with NA authority (a Special Courts Martial Convening Authority); an officer without that authority recommends and does not issue (8.f(1) and (2)).
3. The Marine: label, grade, billet, and the unit by echelon (a rifle company in an infantry battalion, a maintenance platoon in a logistics battalion). The Marine's grade: a Meritorious Mast is for an enlisted Marine (8.g).
4. Has this service or performance already been recognized, or is it being recognized, by a Letter of Appreciation, a Certificate of Commendation, or a personal decoration, or is one being drafted? The order says a Meritorious Mast shall not be conducted in that case (8.g(1)). One recognition per service.

## 2. The facts
5. What did the Marine do? As an event or a period with dates.
6. For how many, how often, how long? Counts, not "numerous" or "countless".
7. What changed because of it, in a number? What did the unit get?
8. Against what? The other Marines of that grade, the unit's standard, last quarter, the inspection before this one.
9. What was the Marine's level of responsibility at the time: the billet held, and whether it was above the grade. Magnitude of the achievement and level of responsibility are the order's two factors for the level (enclosure (2) paragraph 1), and grade is not one (paragraph 6).

## 3. The level
10. With the facts in hand: does this reach "noteworthy or commendable beyond the usual requirements of duty" or show "exceptional industry, judgment, or initiative" (8.g)? If it does not, the recognition is the proficiency and conduct marks and the fitness report, and there is no product.
11. Is it more than that: the kind of achievement enclosure (2) paragraph 1 says warrants a Certificate of Commendation when a Mast or a Letter of Appreciation is not considered appropriate? What in the facts makes it so: magnitude, or level of responsibility?
12. Does it reach "so exceptionally superior that its significance and contribution to the Marine Corps are clearly recognized by superiors and contemporaries alike"? Then it is a personal decoration and the `award` tool, and no Meritorious Mast is held for it (8.g(1)).
13. Would the signer rather thank than commend, or is the recipient not a Marine of this command? Then it is a Letter of Appreciation and the `letter-of-appreciation` tool; it does not enter the OMPF (8.h).

## 4. Routing
14. Who in the unit forwards the copy to CMC (MMSB), and how does this command send it today (paper green copy, or the current electronic system)? The order's routing is paper; record the command's practice in `Overrides/meritorious-mast.md`.
15. For a Certificate of Commendation: are the NAVMC 10631 and 10631A sets on hand (enclosure (1) 9.a), and who adds the social security number to the green copy on the user's computer, after this session?
16. When and where is the presentation, in the presence of Marines (enclosure (1) 5.a)?

## 5. The reader
17. How does this commander want the text written (one paragraph, the order's words in the opening, the closing the command uses)? Record it in `Overrides/meritorious-mast.md`.

## The quantification ladder
| Rung | Question |
|---|---|
| Action | What did the Marine do? |
| Scope | For how many, how often, how long? |
| Result | What changed, in a number? |
| Comparison | Against what: the other Marines of that grade, the unit's standard, last time? |
| Consequence | What did the unit get? |
Stop at the rung the signer can defend to the Marines in the formation who watched it happen.
