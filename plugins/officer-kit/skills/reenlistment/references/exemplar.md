# Reenlistment exemplars, fictional, annotated

Form only. Every number, date, count, and phrase here is invented and is not to be taken into a product; `reenlistment_check.py` fails a draft that lifts a sentence from this file. Read this after the intake is done and the fact list is confirmed, never before, so it teaches shape and supplies no answers. The user's own approved recommendations in `Reference/Exemplars/reenlistment/` beat this file.

## Strong: company commander, block 35d, Recommended with Confidence

```
# Reenlistment recommendation
Marine: <MARINE>, Corporal, 0331, machine gun squad leader   Request: reenlistment   First term   ECC: 19 August 2027   EAS: 19 August 2027
Block: 35d Company Commander   Signed by: the company commander   Date: 17 September 2026
References: MCO 1040.31 enclosure (1) chapter 4 paragraph 4; NAVMC 11537 block 35

## Recommendation
Recommended with Confidence (Top 50 %)

## Population
Corporals known to the certifying officer: 22. Standing: 8 of 22.

## Comments
Conduct: no adverse material on this contract; pro/con average 4.5/4.6 over 31 months. Performance: has led a 7 Marine squad since 2 February 2026; the squad qualified 7 of 7 on the annual gun qualification in June 2026 and was the only one of the company's 9 squads to complete the September 2026 live fire with no safety stoppage. Potential: has run the section's crew drills for two platoons since the section leader detached on 11 July 2026; ready for a sergeant's billet on promotion, not yet for a platoon sergeant's. 8 of 22 places the Marine in the top half, not the top quarter, of the corporals known to me.

## Windows
Initial interview (26 to 24 months before ECC): 19 June 2025 to 19 August 2025. FTAP interview (14 to 12 months before ECC): 19 June 2026 to 19 August 2026. EAS interview (8 to 6 months before EAS): 19 December 2026 to 19 February 2027.
```

Annotation. The tier is stated in the form's words and the population sentence makes it true: 8 of 22 is inside the top half and outside the top quarter, and the last sentence says so, which is what MMEA-6 needs when it sees Confidence rather than Enthusiasm on a Marine with a clean record. The comments follow the order's three heads in the order's order: conduct as a record fact, performance as counts with a comparison (7 of 7; the only one of 9 squads), potential tied to the next grade with the limit stated (a sergeant's billet, not a platoon sergeant's). Every date is a date. Nothing about the decision, which is MMEA-6's. The windows are computed from ECC and EAS so the record shows the FTAP interview fell in its window.
